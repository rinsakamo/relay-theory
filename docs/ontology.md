# Ontology

RelayTheory does not begin from a fixed ontology.

This document tracks the **smallest currently useful candidate basis** and the high-level concepts that may be derivable from it. Every entry is provisional.

## Rule

A name is not a primitive because it is intuitive, implementation-friendly, or common in cognitive language.

Prefer:

```text
named thing
    ↓
relation
    ↓
interaction
    ↓
change
```

when that reduction preserves the distinctions required by evidence.

## Candidate primitives

The following are research candidates, not settled fundamentals.

### Self

A candidate locus for internally continuous state, authority, and relation transformation.

Open questions:

- Must Self be primitive?
- Can Self be derived from a stable boundary / authority closure?
- Which continuity conditions distinguish one Self from another?

### World

The domain outside a given Self boundary that can participate in grounded interaction.

World is relative to the Self boundary under analysis. A Body may therefore be physically in World while remaining a privileged grounding interface for Self.

### Boundary

The distinction governing which state or transformations are internal to Self and which are external.

Open questions include whether Boundary is primitive or derivable from authority / grounding constraints.

### Relation

A structured dependency or connection among states or entities.

Relations should be grounded where possible. Free-form semantic labels must not silently create ontology.

### Interaction

A coupling in which Self, World, or their relations participate in a transition.

Interaction is intentionally lower-level than Action.

### Change

A difference between relevant states or relations across a transition.

Change is intentionally descriptive before it is agentive.

### Grounding

The traceable connection between a representation / relation and the state, observation, body interface, memory, or other source that constrains it.

### Authority

The rule or boundary determining which source may establish, revise, or accept a state or relation.

Authority is not synonymous with ownership.

### Trace

Provenance sufficient to connect changes, grounding sources, authority transitions, and intermediate interactions.

Open question: whether Trace is primitive or merely evidence representation.

## Derived candidates

The following terms are useful descriptions but are **not currently primitive**.

### Perception

Candidate reduction:

> World-to-Self grounded interaction/change that modifies Self-accessible state.

This must distinguish mere World change from change that crosses an embodied or observational boundary.

### Cognition

Candidate reduction:

> Self-to-Self interaction/change that transforms Self-accessible relations, representations, or choice structure.

This definition is provisional. It should not accidentally include physical metabolism merely because metabolism affects the organism.

### Action

Candidate reduction:

> A Self-to-World interaction/change whose relevant outward contribution is authorized and traceable to Self.

Action may therefore be an attribution over interaction/change rather than a primitive event class.

### Ownership

Candidate reduction:

> Attribution derived from Authority + Grounding + Trace over a relevant state or change.

Ownership remains unresolved until counterexamples distinguish it from provenance and authority alone.

### Skill

Candidate reduction:

> A recurrent, reusable, compressed interaction/change pattern.

This allows Skill-like efficiency without making Skill a fundamental runtime object.

### Emotion-like structure

Candidate reduction:

> A recurrent pattern that changes which relations or future interactions remain actionable, often under viability/value constraints.

Whether this requires an independent primitive is unresolved.

## Body

Body is deliberately not equated with Self.

A useful current hypothesis is:

```text
Environment ↔ Body       physical interaction
Body → Self              embodied / interoceptive grounding
Self ↔ Self              cognition-like interaction
Self → Body → World      embodied outward interaction
```

Under this account, metabolism is a World-side physical process until bodily state becomes grounded into Self-accessible state.

This is a hypothesis, not a frozen definition.

## Promotion and deletion

A candidate may be promoted only when a narrower derivation fails to preserve required distinctions.

A candidate should be:

- **derived** when a lower-level account preserves the needed semantics;
- **split** when one term hides multiple mechanisms;
- **weakened** when evidence supports only a subset of its claim;
- **deleted** when it adds no discriminating structure.

Ontology growth is not a success metric.
