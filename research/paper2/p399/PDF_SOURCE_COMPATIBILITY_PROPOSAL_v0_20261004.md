# Proposed unified original-fulltext admission: PDF-first, publisher-HTML fallback (QP07 revised)

**Status:** revised draft following author discussion, for prospective #399 version ratification. **Not yet deployed as a modification to existing v2.3.1**. Historical A/B/C, source labels, Grammar v0, QP01–07, #398 and MAIN permissions remain unchanged.

## Single source-eligibility principle

An **actually readable complete original publication** is scientific authority, regardless of whether presented as publisher PDF or publisher-designated complete original HTML. Prefer the original complete PDF where readable and obtainable during the audit; **if PDF is unavailable, inaccessible or materially unreadable**, admit the publisher-designated complete original **full HTML as an equivalent primary full-text source class**. The equivalence is *scientific source admissibility*, NOT automatically exact textual or image/equation byte identity.

- Confirm title/DOI, published version, update/correction/errata status and relevant model coverage. A publisher-authoritative complete HTML can serve without PDF acquisition, exact HTML-byte retention, PDF parity proof, or requests for standalone linked supplements (consistent with the earlier HTML-only author decision).
- Before A, record the specific primary medium and canonical live URL; PDF records also require original bytes, SHA256, size, page count, readability and equation/figure page locators. Official HTML uses URL, section anchors, in-article equation/figure IDs and in-HTML visibility/readability; record accession date and edition/correction observations. Do not falsely assert persistent HTML immutability when the original site cannot be fixed.
- Choose **one primary rendering/source edition** and keep its original material scope fixed through the A→B→C chain; the other rendering may be a corroborating secondary cross-check. If the source makes the primary unavailable before B/C, do **not** silently swap: record the break, rebind an exact same-edition alternative by a separately documented authorization if justified, otherwise stop or restart the chain.
- Differences noticed between original PDF and official HTML that matter scientifically (missing decisive equation, different update terms, figure labels, published correction not propagated) must be preserved as material source conflicts, not silently harmonized and not automatic evidence of cognitive-grammar residuals. Revisit primary source/edition admission if material.
- Do not elevate third-party mirrors, repository-only HTML or a non-equivalent author manuscript to an official same-edition publisher full-text without separate provenance checks. This does not require exact PDF-vs-HTML parity auditing as a standard qualification step.

## Unified evidence locators

Every assertion and negative finding retains the primary source type and location:
- PDF: `(source_sha256, pdf_page, printed_page_if_any, source_section, local_text_anchor, eq_fig_table_id, text_readability, rendered_visual_check_if_decisive)`.
- Complete original publisher HTML: `(canonical_doi_url, access_date, source_section_anchor, local_text_anchor, visible_original_eq_fig_table_id, in_HTML_visual_readability_if_decisive)`.

For decisive mathematical notation and graphical claims, actually inspect its readable original **in the selected primary medium**. Text extraction from two-column PDFs is navigation, not decisive formula proof. No obligation to procure detached standalone supplements under this proposed standard; claims relying solely on materials outside the accepted primary full-text universe must be excluded or marked underdetermined.

## Shared audit and science

A: decompose the entire frozen full-text primary source; freeze exact complete A and source-located typed edges. B: use **same primary source plus complete frozen A**; challenge all A items/edges and sweep the whole source in order, including zero-objection sections. C: first reread the complete original A and original evidence to prevent duplicate patches; then re-open each objection's source-local passage, capture **every material adjacent negative outcome, failed rescue, counterexample and boundary condition**, adjudicate with source-location and alternative-interpretation checks, and perform a fresh source-order sweep. Only source-supported append-only deltas; material conflicts/decisive unreadability go to targeted exception review, never automatically PASS. D/E use unchanged Grammar v0 and optional separately adopted **derived** coupled System–World recurrence. Do not treat structural toy tests as exact original dynamic or empirical replication.

## What QP07 established versus did not

QP07 used two actual received complete PDF sources. Schurger (2012) original published PDF math is human-legible, and same-article PMC full-text HTML exposes an equation via an embedded image; **publisher PNAS official HTML was unavailable in this development run**. PMC illustrates format pitfalls only, not publisher-PDF parity. Kaelbling et al. (1998) original published 36-page PDF was sufficient for a source-located developmental A/B/C chain and reconstruction. QP07 is **not** a new prospective, unexposed scientific pilot, independent blinded validation, publisher HTML/PDF full equivalence test, full original numerical replication, or authority to retroactively upgrade past experiments.
