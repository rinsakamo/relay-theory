# M9-B — Single-adjudicator and independence boundary

Independent human re-adjudication was **not performed**.

The study therefore establishes **procedural auditability rather than inter-rater reliability**. No Cohen's kappa, independent-coder agreement, or equivalent reliability statistic is claimed.

The following are explicitly **not** substitutes for independent human adjudication:
- LLM-assisted checks or separate model sessions,
- deterministic CI,
- Lean formal verification,
- M9-A synthetic/matched calibration,
- source-identity separation,
- genealogy analysis.

Those procedures address different failure modes.

What is claimed instead:
1. exact roster and no-substitution rule are frozen;
2. source-first decomposition, source re-audit, adjudication, reconstruction, and final outcome provenance are inspectable;
3. A0/A1/A2 has an operational decision tree;
4. all 40 paper-level outcomes expose exact lane provenance and source-loci artifact locations;
5. M9-A shows A1/A2 are reachable and rules out a purely vocabulary-forced A0 outcome;
6. genealogy uncertainty is explicit and the 40 papers are not treated as independent statistical replications.

Manuscript-safe wording:

> Independent human re-adjudication was not performed. Accordingly, the present study establishes procedural auditability rather than inter-rater reliability. LLM-assisted checks, deterministic CI, formal verification, and synthetic calibration are not counted as independent human adjudication. The paper-level evidence table is supplied so that an external researcher can conduct a genuinely independent re-adjudication without relying on the aggregate labels reported here.

Preferred description of MAIN40:
**single-adjudicator, source-identity-separated prospective compatibility test under a frozen reconstruction contract**.
