# M13 Beni dialectical reconstruction

Status: SOURCE_VERIFIED_DIALECTICAL_RECONSTRUCTION

Primary source: Majid D. Beni, “Kinds Without Structure Are Blind; Structure Without Kinds Is Empty: Remarks on the Methodology of Consciousness Science,” Journal for General Philosophy of Science (2026), DOI 10.1007/s10838-025-09759-z.

Verified version of record: Springer Nature HTML, checked 2026-10-06.

## Strongest relevant argument

1. Heterogeneous consciousness theories need not remain merely juxtaposed; a sufficiently explicit structural framework can integrate them.
2. Beni proposes predictive processing / FEP as a formally coherent candidate framework for exposing invariant relational organization across otherwise divergent theories.
3. The structural realist proposal treats such invariant organization as methodologically relevant to convergence and, explicitly, to kind individuation.
4. Therefore, if the preserved relations are genuinely the relevant invariant organization, the common structure can carry kind-individuating evidential weight.

## What M13 accepts

- Structural unification can reveal non-obvious commonalities.
- Invariance can be epistemically important for ontology-building.
- A formal framework should be explicit enough to state what is preserved.
- PP/FEP is a legitimate candidate for this role; M13 does not argue against PP/FEP.
- Beni explicitly does not present PP/FEP as the final or uniquely correct account.

## Where M13 adds a burden

Successful reconstruction plus observed invariance does not by itself establish that the preserved relation is kind- or capacity-constitutive. Before the structure bears identity weight, an additional reason must connect the chosen representation, transformation class, preservation criterion, and granularity to the identity question.

Possible dischargers include:
- mechanistic constitution or organization;
- causal difference-making;
- empirical invariance under independently relevant interventions or contexts;
- explanatory indispensability or explanatory-role stability;
- an independently defended objective/non-pragmatic perspicuity relation of the North type.

The claim is conditional and non-hostile: Beni supplies a concrete structuralist project for which M13's distinction between specification and identity-relevant warrant matters.
