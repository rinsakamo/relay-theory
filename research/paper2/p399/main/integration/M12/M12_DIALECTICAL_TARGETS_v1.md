# M12 dialectical targets

The paper does not need a philosopher who literally says "labels alone settle individuation." Its target is three stronger inference forms that can occur in ontology-building and structural unification.

## 1. Terminological Carryover Inference (TCI)

**Form:** repeated or shared inherited construct terminology itself contributes evidence of capacity identity or a stable boundary across sources.

**M12 result:** label continuity may organize inquiry but cannot discharge structural identity. MEM04/CNC05 show why: the same historical phrase "semantic memory" occupies different claim roles and their preserved bounded supports are disjoint under both the working basis and CPCG.

**Consequence:** the unified-capacity thesis remains open, but it inherits explicit preservation debt.

## 2. Reconstruction-to-Identity Inference (RII)

**Form:** if heterogeneous source models can be reconstructed within one formal framework without extra mechanisms, successful unification itself supports privilege or identity for that framework's capacity structure.

**M12 result:** under encoding-permissive semantics, the full basis and six weaker views all keep MAIN40 at 40/40 A0. Successful reconstruction is therefore insufficient for basis selection.

**Relation to Beni:** Beni's 2026 structural-realist proposal uses predictive processing as a candidate formal structure for identifying invariant relations across consciousness theories. M12 does not reject that candidate. It states an upstream evidential burden: unification success alone must be supplemented by an argument for the relevant preservation/invariance class or representational privilege.

## 3. Local-Invariance Promotion (LIP)

**Form:** invariance within a chosen representation/transformation class is promoted without further argument to representation-independent capacity identity.

**M12 result:** TAIF changes first-class factorization losslessly; CPCG destroys substantial fine structure while preserving substantial higher-order reuse. "Invariant" therefore needs a declared transformation class.

**Relation to North:** North argues that some physical representations can be objectively, non-pragmatically more perspicuous. M12 does not deny this possibility. Rather, objective perspicuity would be one way to discharge a burden that encoding success cannot discharge. M12 remains agnostic about whether its working basis is objectively perspicuous.

**Relation to Brousalis:** Brousalis makes epistemically relevant similarities/differences central to comparative methodology. M12 supplies a bounded operational precondition: the dimensions and preservation rule of comparison must be explicit before similarity/difference can carry evidential weight.

**Relation to Onishi & Serpico:** preservation criteria and system boundaries may remain inquiry-relative. M12 exposes and sensitivity-tests that relativity rather than claiming to eliminate it.
