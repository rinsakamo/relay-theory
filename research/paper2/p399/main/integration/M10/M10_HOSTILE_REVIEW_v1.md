## 1. Representation dependence

**Attack:** The nine-role grammar could be an artifact of one chosen factorization.

**Current response:** The TAIF rival basis is frozen separately and losslessly reorganizes the same structural information into five first-class families while preserving indexed distinctions. Deterministic guards round-trip 9/9 Grammar-v0 roles, 8/8 working-basis axes, 60/60 reverse-projection role sets, and 206/206 bounded objects; 99 cross-stratum families and the 1770/1770 whole-claim result remain preserved.

**Judgment:** The exact first-class inventory is constructively shown not to be representation-invariant. The higher-order comparison conclusions survive one plausible lossless refactorization. The manuscript correctly does not infer representation neutrality.

**Residual limitation:** Only one lossless rival factorization is tested. Substantively different lossy or alternative semantic representations remain future adversarial tests.

## 2. Interest-relativity

**Attack:** Boundary choice and preservation criteria may simply encode the investigator's interests.

**Current response:** Related Work now engages Onishi & Serpico on interest-relative category delineation, while the system--environment section keeps system, environment, coupling, experiment, and source context distinct. The text explicitly states that these choices are exposed rather than eliminated.

**Judgment:** The objection is bounded rather than dissolved, which is the correct claim. The procedure is auditable with respect to declared interests; it is not interest-free.

## 3. Adjudicator dependence

**Attack:** A single author plus LLM assistance cannot establish inter-rater reliability.

**Current response:** Independent human re-adjudication is explicitly NOT PERFORMED. The manuscript states: "Procedural auditability is established; inter-rater reliability remains unmeasured." LLM checks, deterministic CI, Lean, synthetic calibration, rival-basis sensitivity, and perturbation controls are explicitly not substitutes.

**Judgment:** This remains an open validation limitation, not a hidden methodological claim.

## 4. A0 triviality

**Attack:** If all source mechanisms may simply be retained, a sufficiently general grammar could make almost every real model A0.

**Current response:** In addition to prior synthetic reachability, two counterfactual controls start from exact frozen real-source-derived prospective structures. INT-10 loses a source-defined typed mediator and requires an A1 stateless bridge. INT-16 loses a source-defined history-bearing eligibility mechanism and requires A2 persistent state. Certified MAIN40 remains untouched at 40/0/0.

**Judgment:** The controls refute an absorbing-A0 classifier semantics on realistic source-derived structures.

**Residual limitation:** The ablations are deliberately constructed load-bearing counterfactuals. They do not show that naturally occurring A1/A2 cases are common, nor do they provide prevalence evidence.

## 5. Positive philosophical contribution

**Attack:** "Labels do not guarantee identity" risks sounding trivial.

**Current response:** The revision now makes the positive claim an evidential-burden rule: a structural identity/difference claim must specify (i) a declared comparison representation, (ii) an explicit preservation criterion, and (iii) the granularity of comparison. Whole-claim incomparability and bounded reuse are therefore compatible answers to different declared questions. The method is positioned as a comparison/pre-individuation procedure prior to stronger natural-kind, mechanistic, or invariance-based individuation.

**Judgment:** This is the clearest statement of what the protocol establishes philosophically. It is stronger than a warning about terminology but remains weaker than a universal ontology.
