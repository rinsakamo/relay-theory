# MAIN M25 — Question-relative defeater and process-level sensitivity witness

M25 responds to the post-M24 JGPS-style review without changing the frozen scientific results or adding a new corpus execution.

## Core philosophical change

A representational disagreement is defeater-relevant only after the identity question **Q** and individuation criterion **C** are held fixed. Different representations can answer different legitimate questions and both be correct. Representation sensitivity alone is therefore not yet a Representational-Defeater Trigger.

The Discrimination Requirement is also expressed in terms of a **substantial evidential role** rather than an unspecified material-weight threshold. A structural similarity is substantial when, holding the rest of the stated evidential package fixed, removing or substantially weakening that similarity removes a needed premise or requires replacement evidence to preserve the same level of support.

## Capacity identity

The paper targets type-level cognitive-capacity identity. It does not impose one universal individuation criterion. Functional, mechanistic, or other scientifically motivated criteria may be used, but the criterion must be declared before a representation is treated as a defeater-relevant rival.

## Botvinick--Tulving

The old zero-fine / positive-coarse result is unchanged. Its M25 role is a boundary case. Botvinick's temporal structure is process-dynamic; Tulving's source commitment includes past-directed subjective time, while history_window is an analysis-level encoding. Their coarse/fine difference shows why different temporal questions should not be treated as same-question rivals by default.
