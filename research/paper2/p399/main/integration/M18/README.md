# M18 — Beni abductive evidence and source-first triangulation

M18 is a philosophical presentation revision stacked on the current M17 JGPS-facing manuscript.

It does not change any scientific result.

The central clarification is:

- explanatory/unificatory success is genuine abductive evidence for a candidate framework;
- the evidential question is what that success discriminates;
- when a candidate framework is selected first and heterogeneous theories are redescribed within it, successful unification may reflect source-side structure, framework-enabled accommodation, or both;
- therefore reconstruction success may contribute to the warrant for individuation, but the warrant cannot be exhausted by that same success;
- additional discriminating support may come from prediction, intervention, mechanistic evidence, contrastive framework comparison, objective perspicuity, or convergence with a differently constructed reconstruction route.

Beni is treated as a positive interlocutor, not as committing a simple fallacy.

Paper 2 is described as source-first only in the limited relevant sense: source-local commitments are reconstructed before PP-FEP comparison and are not generated by first translating every source into PP-FEP vocabulary. The method is explicitly not framework-free, theory-neutral, or representation-neutral.

The positive proposal is epistemic triangulation:

- framework-guided route: candidate framework -> redescribed theories -> candidate invariants;
- source-first route: source theories -> structured reconstruction -> recurrent organization.

Convergence would strengthen robustness; divergence would identify where a candidate framework contributes additional organization.

A systematic PP-FEP alignment is future comparative work and is not claimed as a completed result.
