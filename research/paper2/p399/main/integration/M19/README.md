# M19 — Discrimination Requirement consolidation

M19 does not add a new experiment and does not change any scientific result.

It turns the M18 abductive-under-determination argument into one named philosophical contribution:

**Discrimination Requirement**

When an invariant exposed by structural comparison is used as evidence for cognitive-capacity identity, the evidential case must discriminate source-side recurrence from commonality introduced or amplified by the representation, preservation rule, or granularity through which the comparison is made.

The revision also makes the claim-to-capacity bridge explicit:

claim-level recurrent structure
+ target alignment
+ capacity-relevant bridge warrant
+ representational discrimination
-> defeasible evidence for capacity identity.

Beni remains the clearest positive application, but a direct PP-FEP/source-first alignment is future probative work rather than a premise needed for the general argument.

The lossy representation witness is not promoted to a uniquely correct coarse representation. It is defended only as a scientifically intelligible preservation alternative under which the overlap verdict changes.

The prospective source study and named-model replay are further subordinated to procedural-diagnostic roles; detailed counts remain in the supplement.
