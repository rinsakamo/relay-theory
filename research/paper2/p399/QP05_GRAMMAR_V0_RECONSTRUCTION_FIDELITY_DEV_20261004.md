## Source-to-Grammar v0 reconstruction

- **Pi/X**: World-supplied context, independently representable goal/reward and keyboard-to-movement mapping, clustered latent structure, separate inferential posteriors and retained expert/strategy weights. Joint clusters reward+mapping as ONE context cluster, while Independent clusters the two separately.
- **C/Q/K/T**: A CRP prior counts **distinct contexts**, not repeat trials in one context. Observation-dependent posterior and MAP approximation produce learned mapping and reward estimates. Meta adds **persistently updated source-defined** reward-prediction-based weights over Joint/Independent and samples the strategies according to their weights. Internal strategy-state succession differs from World-context scheduling. This extra source-defined state is essential relative to a baseline consisting only of two fixed, uncoordinated strategies; it is **not** a newly invented missing primitive.
- **P/rho-O and planner**: Both generalizing strategies share a source-specified grid-world model-based planner, value iteration/Bellman equations, softmax direction choice followed by learned keyboard mapping. - **Explicit source-level comparator differences**: Flat does not pool context data; `alpha→∞` is a **limiting analogy**, not evidence Flat uses the CRP generative mechanism.
