## Revised novelty statement

### Established prior art

Paper 0 treats the following as inherited methodology: conceptual explication; counterexample-guided abstraction/refinement; analyst-choice and robustness analysis; preregistration/Registered Reports; relational structure mapping; Formal Concept Analysis; ontology matching; bibliometric science mapping; systematic-review provenance; measurement invariance; severe-testing traditions; formal refinement and forgetting; and held-out/out-of-sample separation as a general methodological pattern.

### Borrowed and combined

The protocol borrows these elements and couples them in a fixed temporal order: adversarial construction of a bounded working basis; pre-outcome freezing of the representational/comparison contract; source-grounded decomposition; construct-label/authority blinding while retaining source semantics; bounded structural mapping with explicit equivalence/refinement/incomparability/residual semantics; anti-vacuity controls; and held-out evaluation of the frozen system.

### Smallest surviving contribution

> A source-grounded scientific-claim comparison protocol that orders and couples (i) adversarial construction of a bounded working basis, (ii) pre-outcome freezing of the representational and comparison contract, (iii) construct-label/authority-blinded but source-semantics-grounded decomposition and mapping, (iv) explicit equivalence/refinement/incomparability/residual semantics under bounded expressivity, and (v) held-out checks for coverage, stability, leakage, discrimination, and bespoke-encoding pressure.

This is an **integration/protocol contribution**. It is not a component-method novelty claim.

### Paper 0 does not claim

- historical firstness;
- a uniquely correct or complete ontology/basis;
- semantics-free scientific interpretation;
- that structural equivalence implies semantic/metaphysical identity;
- that pre-outcome freezing eliminates judgment;
- superiority over the strongest near-neighbors;
- demonstrated domain-general validity;
- that AI is a scientific authority or novelty source.
