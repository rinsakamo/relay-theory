\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} cognitive ontology; structural comparison; cognitive capacities; pre-individuation; representation; philosophy of cognitive science


\begin{abstract}

Scientific vocabularies organize cognitive research into capacities such as memory, learning, skill, attention, prediction, control, belief, and concept. This paper asks what evidential work must occur before such labels can support claims about capacity identity or difference. The central thesis is that construct-based individuation is evidentially incomplete unless the comparison representation, preservation criterion, and granularity are independently stated; neither terminology nor successful reconstruction licenses representation-independent identity. The direct objects of analysis are source-grounded scientific claims about capacities, not capacities themselves. A designed 60-claim stress-test corpus was therefore compared with construct labels suppressed at the comparison stage. Whole-claim comparison left all 1,770 pairs incomparable, whereas a separately declared bounded reconstruction recovered 206 reusable structural subobjects and 99 cross-stratum families. Reverse projection yielded a recurrent structural-role grammar with no residual case establishing a new top-level role. A pre-specified 40-paper prospective compatibility test then yielded A0=40, A1=0, A2=0 under the full basis. In a new discriminative closure control, the full basis retained 40/40 A0, whereas a dynamic backbone, a POMDP-like view, and four single-role ablations each shifted all 40 cases to A1 under the same direct-preservation least-lift rule. A genuinely lossy rival representation compressed 206 bounded objects to 143 signatures and collapsed 63 of 99 cross-stratum families, yet retained 62 cross-stratum signatures and cross-stratum support for 58/60 claims. These results support a pre-individuation evidential constraint, not a natural-kind theory, prevalence estimate, universal architecture, or representation-neutral ontology. Independent human re-adjudication was not performed.

\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Cognitive science inherits a large vocabulary of capacities: memory, attention, learning, belief, skill, prediction, control, concepts, and many others. These labels are indispensable for communication and literature organization, but they can also enter an argument too early. A stable name can make heterogeneous scientific claims appear to concern one already-individuated capacity, while different names can make structurally overlapping claims appear to concern different capacities.

The construct-validity tradition already denies that a construct validates itself by being named \citep{CronbachMeehl1955ConstructValidity,BorsboomMellenberghVanHeerden2004Validity}, and cognitive-ontology projects explicitly represent relations among concepts, tasks, and measurements \citep{PoldrackEtAl2011CognitiveAtlas,TurnerLaird2012CogPO,PoldrackYarkoni2016CognitiveOntologies}. The unresolved methodological question is what evidence is required before comparisons among scientific claims can bear on capacity individuation.

The paper's central thesis is therefore stronger than the observation that labels can mislead: \emph{Construct-based individuation is evidentially incomplete unless the comparison representation, preservation criterion, and granularity are independently stated; neither terminology nor successful reconstruction licenses representation-independent identity.} The thesis concerns an evidential burden. It does not claim that capacities are unreal, that one structural language is metaphysically privileged, or that a single comparison procedure can settle a natural-kind question.

This distinction also fixes the level of analysis. The objects directly represented here are source-grounded \emph{scientific claims about cognitive capacities}. The inferential bridge is
\[
\text{claim-level structural comparison}
\longrightarrow
\text{evidential constraint on capacity comparison}
\longrightarrow
\text{capacity individuation}.
\]
The present procedure operates on the first arrow. It does not individuate capacities directly; it specifies conditions that structural evidence should satisfy before it is used in the second step.

That burden matters to several live approaches without requiring any of them to endorse the same ontology. Mechanistic cognitive ontology faces operationalization, abstraction, and boundary problems \citep{FranckenSlorsCraver2022CognitiveOntology}. Invariance-based individuation asks when mechanisms and capacities remain the same across contexts \citep{WajnermanPazRojasLibano2026Bidimensional}. Natural-kind proposals remain sensitive to the interests by which causal clusters and category boundaries are selected \citep{OnishiSerpico2022HPC}. More general work on representation and theory comparison emphasizes that representational choice and the epistemic purpose of comparison can matter to what counts as a perspicuous or relevant similarity \citep{North2026Perspicuous,Brousalis2026TheoryComparison}. Beni's structural-realist proposal for consciousness science likewise shows how a candidate structural framework can be used to reorganize debates about kinds \citep{Beni2026KindsWithoutStructure}. The present paper is prior to these stronger projects in one specific sense: before a vocabulary-defined or structurally unified grouping is used as evidence for capacity identity, the representation, preservation rule, and comparison granularity must be made inspectable.

Three consequences organize the analysis. First, vocabulary does not settle individuation: label agreement and label disagreement are both defeasible. Second, a failed comparison can be informative if its granularity is preserved rather than silently relaxed; whole-claim incomparability may diagnose excessive preservation rather than global incommensurability. Third, successful reconstruction is representation-relative. Robustness must therefore be distinguished from mere invertible recoding and tested against plausible information-losing rivals.

The method follows those consequences. Historical construct labels and selected source-authority metadata are suppressed at the comparison stage, while source-grounded claim structure is retained. A whole-claim comparison is performed first and its failure is preserved. A separately declared bounded-reuse question is then asked. The recovered structure is reverse-projected into a compact role grammar, attacked by residual and comparator tests, and finally evaluated on a separately fixed 40-paper prospective corpus. The present revision adds two missing discriminative checks: the prospective result is recomputed as a closure control under weaker comparison bases, and the bounded-reuse result is projected into a genuinely lossy rival representation.

The procedure is therefore not neutral among all theories or representations. It is \emph{construct-label-suppressed at the comparison stage}: inherited labels do not enter the structural similarity calculation, but the comparison representation and preservation criterion are explicit methodological choices.

\section{Methods}

\subsection{Designed corpus}

The bounded empirical target contains 60 claims. Forty-eight primary claims are divided evenly across eight historical construct strata. Twelve additional claims form a non-tuning challenge surface.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering

\label{tab:corpus}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\toprule

\midrule
Primary & 8 strata \(\times\) 6 claims & 48 \\
Challenge & 12 predeclared pressure classes & 12 \\
\midrule
Total & & 60 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

The primary strata are Memory, Learning, Skill, Attention, Prediction, Control, Belief, and Concept. Challenge pressures include multi-timescale organization, stochastic or approximate structure, constitutive boundary pressure, compositional or systematic structure, embodied or world-coupled organization, normative or criterion structure, hybrid labels, label-coordinate mismatch, multi-condition structure, same-inventory/different-topology pressure, strict-refinement pressure, and high structural heterogeneity. Challenge claims were not permitted to tune the structured-record schema, working basis, or comparison semantics.

This is a literature-based structural comparison, not an experiment or prevalence study. Its role is instead adversarial and structural: the 48 primary claims provide balanced coverage across historically familiar construct strata, while the 12 challenge claims probe predeclared pressures that could break the representation. Consequently, the supported inference is whether reusable structure survives this designed stress-test surface under pre-specified rules, not how prevalent any role or reusable structural subobject is in cognitive science as a whole.

\subsection{Source-grounded structured claim records}

Each selected source claim was represented in a machine-readable structured claim record containing stable source identity, source-local evidence locators, claim scope and modality, typed nodes, typed relations, temporal information, control information, approximation state, and bridge assumptions.

A language model assisted initial structured extraction, but its output was not treated as authority. Every retained record was reviewed against source text, and all 60 current records carry reviewed status. The scientific input to downstream analysis is therefore the source-audited structured claim corpus.

Independent extraction reliability was not established. Historical local two-pass calibration transactions produced zero valid structured claim record records in both the v2 and v3 real calibration runs, leaving no valid A/B agreement denominator. the present study consequently claims auditability of the retained corpus, not inter-model agreement, autonomous extraction reliability, or deterministic language-model replay.

\subsection{Pre-specification and anti-circularity}

The comparison basis, whole-claim projection, and bounded-reconstruction rules were fixed before later grammar and prospective outcomes were used to interpret them. Reverse projection did not reread source papers or rewrite the reviewed claim records to improve grammar coverage, and residuals were retained rather than repaired after inspection. The purpose of this staging is not to remove author judgment, which remains present in source review and structural adjudication, but to prevent the final grammar from simultaneously defining and validating its own input representation. Exact repository chronology, immutable hashes, and stage-level provenance are reported in the Supplementary Methods rather than used as argumentative premises in the main text.

\subsection{Generative AI and human accountability}

OpenAI ChatGPT assisted structured extraction, source-analysis workflow, software development, and manuscript drafting. Model output was not treated as source authority or as an independent adjudicator. Every retained claim in the 60-claim reference corpus was reviewed by the author against source text, and the prospective study tied adjudications to explicit source locations and versioned artifacts. Independent extraction reliability and independent human re-adjudication were not established; these remain distinct limitations. Full disclosure and workflow provenance are provided in the Supplementary Methods.

\subsection{Pre-specified structural basis}

The structural comparison suppresses source information that should not determine similarity merely by appearing in a paper. Author identity, institution, venue, citation count, historical construct label, source-local identifiers, and free-text presentation are excluded from comparison.

The pre-specified working basis is
\[
B_{P2}=\{S,\Pi,K,O,T,C,Q,P\},
\]
interpreted as a declared structural coordinate system for comparison rather than an ontology or a representation-neutral basis. Lexical shortcuts are forbidden: a source node named \emph{condition} is not automatically \(C\); a reward-like term does not exhaust \(Q\); temporal precedence is not automatically \(K\); and a partition need not be physical.

\subsection{Whole-claim comparison}

The pre-specified \(\Phi\) projection preserves claim type, modality, scope shape, typed topology, active basis coordinates, temporal presence states, control states, approximation mode, and bridge-grounding multiplicity. It forgets source authority and presentation metadata.

Pairwise relations distinguish equivalence, refinement, forgetting, incomparability, and mutual-embed non-isomorphism. For 60 claims, the unordered comparison surface contains
\[
\binom{60}{2}=1770
\]
pairs.

\subsection{Secondary bounded reconstruction}

Because whole-claim comparison failed universally, the next analysis changed the \emph{unit of reuse}, not the pre-specified source representation. The whole-claim result therefore remains the primary result for the original \(\Phi\)-level question. A secondary post-atlas reconstruction then asked a different question: whether bounded substructures could recur even when complete scientific claims remained incomparable. In that chronological sense the reconstruction is post-atlas; it is not presented as if it had been the successful primary comparison from the outset.

The bounded reconstruction searched for basis-subobjects recoverable under a pre-specified forgetting operator. The operator permits restriction to retained typed substructure but forbids arbitrary rewiring and semantic relabeling. Thus the secondary analysis does not revise the 1,770 whole-claim verdicts, and successful bounded recurrence cannot be used to retrospectively relabel a failed whole-claim pair as equivalent.

Negative and ablation controls tested trivial-core collapse, label dependence, permissive forgetting, richer competing cores, overlapping incomparable cores, cross-label recurrence, compatibility with whole-claim incomparability, and presentation invariance. No post-outcome abstraction threshold was added to manufacture recurrence.

\subsection{Structural role grammar and adversarial tests}

Surviving bounded structures motivated a compact structural role grammar (version 0):
\[
\Gsys=\{\Pi,X,C,Q,\Pin,\Pout,K,T,\rho/O\}.
\]
Here \(X\) is a generic configuration carrier; \(C\) is realizability/admissibility structure; \(Q\) is criterion-conditioned orientation; \(\Pin\) and \(\Pout\) are directional interface roles; \(K\) is typed transformation or dependence; \(T\) is succession or temporal indexing; and \(\rho/O\) is boundary-relative observation or trace.

A mnemonic representation is
\[
X_{n+1}=K_{n;C,Q,\Pin}(X_n),
\qquad
O_n=\rho_{\Pi,C,\Pout,n}(X_n),
\]
without assuming that every claim instantiates every role.

All 60 reviewed claims were reverse-projected into this structural role grammar without rereading source papers or changing the structured claim records, \(B_{P2}\), \(\Phi\), structural adjudications, or reusable-subobject identities. Residuals were classified under a pre-specified taxonomy in which only an unexplained requirement for an additional top-level system role counted as prima facie evidence for extending the role inventory.

Two strict distinction-preservation comparators were tested:
\[
G_{\mathrm{dyn}}=\{X,K,T\},
\qquad
G_{\mathrm{pomdp}}=\{X,P,K,\rho/O,Q,T\}.
\]
These are distinction-loss tests, not non-encodability theorems.


\subsection{Prospective 40-paper compatibility test}

After the structural role grammar and original 60-claim analyses were held fixed, a separate no-substitution roster of 40 cognitive-model papers was analyzed: 24 component-oriented papers and 16 integrated papers selected to expose cross-mechanism composition. The exact DOIs have zero overlap with the original 60-claim corpus, although genealogical independence is not inferred from DOI separation.

The decision variable is the \emph{least reconstruction-added structure required for source-faithful reconstruction}. Let \(\mathcal R_0\hookrightarrow\mathcal R_1\hookrightarrow\mathcal R_2\) denote nested reconstruction classes permitting, respectively, source-defined mechanisms only, reconstruction-added stateless mediation, and reconstruction-added persistent/stateful mediation. With \(E_i\) the source contracts faithfully realizable at level \(i\),
\[
A0=E_0,\qquad
A1=E_1\setminus E_0,\qquad
A2=E_2\setminus E_1.
\]
Source-defined memory, hierarchy, recurrence, gating, planner state, or other persistent structure can therefore remain A0. A1 requires a load-bearing reconstruction-added typed/stateless interface, translation, or sequencing structure; A2 requires an added load-bearing persistent/stateful mechanism after all source-defined structure and possible stateless repairs are accounted for. Ineligible or evidentially unresolved sources are not forced into an A-state.

This was a single-adjudicator compatibility test, not an inter-rater reliability study. Independent human re-adjudication was not performed. The full paper-level evidence table and exact stage chronology are supplied in the Supplementary Methods and public provenance artifacts.

\subsection{Prospective discriminative closure controls}

The observed \(40/40\) A0 result creates a specific validity question: would a substantially weaker comparison basis also classify all 40 papers as A0 simply because source-defined mechanisms are retained? To test that possibility without changing any certified source adjudication, the same frozen 40 paper-level structural records were evaluated under seven declared comparison bases:
\[
\begin{aligned}
G_{\mathrm{full}}&=\{\Pi,X,C,Q,P,K,T,\rho/O\},\\
G_{\mathrm{dyn}}&=\{X,K,T\},\\
G_{\mathrm{pomdp}}&=\{X,P,K,Q,T,\rho/O\},
\end{aligned}
\]
together with four single-role ablations of \(G_{\mathrm{full}}\) removing \(\Pi\), \(C\), \(P\), or \(T\).

The same direct-preservation least-lift rule is used in every arm. If a source-required frozen distinction remains first-class, no additional structure is charged. If it is omitted but can be re-exposed by a stateless typed boundary, constraint, criterion, interface, observation, or sequencing structure whose content is already source-defined, the comparator closure state is A1. No omitted distinction may be hidden in opaque generic model code, and no new source semantics may be invented.

This control is deliberately transparent rather than presented as a blinded experiment. Before the control was frozen, the existing prospective matrix already showed that all 40 accepted reconstructions instantiated all eight working-basis coordinates. The control therefore tests the logical discriminability of the declared preservation contract on the frozen prospective structures; it does not constitute an independent source re-adjudication.

\subsection{Lossless non-uniqueness and a genuinely lossy rival representation}

Two different representation-sensitivity questions are kept separate. First, an invertible Tagged Admissibility--Interface Factorization (TAIF) tests whether the exact nine first-class roles are unique when the same information is losslessly refactored. Second, a Coarse Process--Constraint Graph (CPCG) asks whether higher-order bounded-reuse conclusions survive a deliberately information-losing representation.

CPCG has five coarse families: \(N\) for carrier/component structure, merging state-like and partition/boundary structure; \(G\) for guard/orientation, merging constraints and criteria; \(I\) for interface events, merging probes/interventions with observations/readouts; \(E\) for non-temporal transformation/dependence; and \(T\) for temporal presence/order. Fine node-role strings, working-basis control subtypes, non-temporal relation kinds, and detailed temporal subtypes are discarded. The map is therefore non-injective by construction. No retention threshold was declared: survival and collapse are both reportable sensitivity outcomes.

\section{Results}

\subsection{Whole-claim comparison fails}
\label{sec:wholefail}

The pre-specified whole-claim result was:
\[
1770/1770=\text{incomparable}.
\]

Across 3,540 directed comparisons, 3,518 failed at the scalar stage, six reached node-count comparison, 16 reached node-label multiset comparison, and none reached topology or embedding. Universal incomparability was therefore localized primarily to the enriched pre-topology comparison surface rather than to graph topology itself.

This negative result is retained as evidence rather than removed from the final narrative.

\subsection{Bounded reusable structure reappears}

The secondary bounded reconstruction produced 213 within-stratum candidate subobjects and 206 unique reusable structural subobjects. Seven exact cross-stratum equivalence components survived. The global refinement structure contains 470 direct refinement edges and 2,894 strict subobject relations in transitive closure.

The bounded family structure contains
\[
99\ \text{cross-stratum families}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
107\ \text{within-stratum-only families},
\]
with 188 distinct support signatures. Fifty-nine of 60 claims are covered by at least one surviving bounded object. All eight negative and ablation controls pass.

Notably, 1,304 whole-claim pairs share at least one surviving reusable structural subobject despite every whole-claim pair being held as incomparable. Whole-claim identity and reusable substructure are therefore distinct questions on the same corpus.

Generic dynamical cognition is established prior art \citep{VanGelder1998DynamicalHypothesis}, and contemporary dynamical cognitive science explicitly emphasizes temporally extended brain--body--environment organization \citep{FavelaRaja2026Dynamical}. The result here is not that dynamical models are invalid. Likewise, canonical POMDPs provide state, action, transition, observation, and reward/criterion organization for partially observable control \citep{KaelblingLittmanCassandra1998POMDP}.

These comparators are not fitted predictive models, performance benchmarks, or statistical competitors. No reward optimization, prior/posterior tuning, predictive-accuracy comparison, or parameter fitting is performed. The test is strictly representational: under the declared forgetful projection, which distinctions remain first-class and which become implicit or disappear? The result is therefore only that the tested natural projections omit distinctions explicitly preserved by the pre-specified corpus; it does not show that enriched dynamical or POMDP formalisms are incapable of encoding them. In that limited sense, the reason for not adopting the POMDP-like view as the comparison basis is not insufficient generic encoding power but excessive forgetting relative to distinctions from the source models that this study had held fixed as first-class.

\subsection{Non-uniqueness control: lossless TAIF}

The Tagged Admissibility--Interface Factorization (TAIF) reorganizes the same frozen information into five first-class families,
\[
G_{\mathrm{TAIF}}=\{X,A,I,K,T\}.
\]
The \(A\) family carries indexed boundary, constraint, and criterion subtypes, while \(I\) carries indexed input, output, and observation subtypes. The mapping is explicitly invertible. It round-trips all nine role atoms, all eight working-basis axes, all 60 reverse-projection role sets, and all 206 bounded objects while preserving the 99 cross-stratum support relation and the 1,770/1,770 whole-claim result.

TAIF therefore establishes \emph{non-uniqueness of first-class factorization}, not strong robustness. Because it is an invertible recoding, preservation of higher-order results is expected once the subtype indices are retained. Its philosophical role is negative but important: success of the original grammar does not license representation-independent privilege for its exact nine-role inventory.

\subsection{Lossy-rival sensitivity: CPCG}

CPCG supplies the stronger representation-sensitivity test because it intentionally discards distinctions. The 206 bounded structural objects collapse to 143 distinct CPCG signatures, with 37 collision groups among originally distinct archetypes. Of the original 99 cross-stratum families, 63 (63.6\%) collide with at least one other original family under the coarse projection.

Despite that loss, reusable cross-stratum organization does not disappear. Sixty-two CPCG signatures retain support from more than one sampling stratum, and 58 of the 60 source claims participate in at least one such cross-stratum signature. The ATT01--BLF01 cross-label witness remains shared after projection. Thus the exact 206-object and 99-family inventories are representation-sensitive, while a substantial higher-order reuse pattern survives one non-invertible rival.

This result still falls short of representation neutrality. CPCG is one plausible coarse process representation, not an exhaustive space of rivals, and the survival counts are descriptive rather than a natural-kind criterion.

\subsection{Post hoc discriminability stress test of the A0/A1/A2 classification rule}

An adversarial calibration performed after the prospective 40-paper analysis tested whether the A0/A1/A2 classification rule itself was structurally attracted to A0. This calibration is explicitly post hoc and does not alter or re-adjudicate any prospective paper.

The A0/A1/A2 rule was expressed in terms of the minimal faithful reconstruction level within the nested reconstruction classes above and tested on two matched synthetic families: a strict dynamical family and a POMDP-like family. Each family contains one direct case, one typed stateless-bridge case, and one history-sensitive persistent-bridge case. The six expected outcomes are therefore two A0, two A1, and two A2 cases, and all six classes were reached as constructed.

The A1 witness uses incompatible declared port types: direct composition fails, while an explicit stateless map repairs the mismatch. The A2 witness requires the bridge output to depend on the immediately preceding binary input. No current-input-only function can satisfy both histories that share the same present input but require different outputs; a one-bit persistent bridge can. The corresponding Lean formalization verifies the minimal-reconstruction partition, the positive A1 witness, the stateless obstruction for A2, the stateful A2 realization, and the factorization of structural role grammar forgetting through the POMDP-like view to the strict dynamical transition view.

This calibration establishes reachability of A1 and A2 under the pre-specified reconstruction-addition semantics. It does not establish independent-human adjudication reliability, empirical frequency of the three outcomes, or non-encodability of the tested structures in richer dynamical or POMDP formalisms.

Crucially, absence of a discovered path, citation, or common constituent was never promoted to genealogical independence. The 40 outcomes are therefore reported as raw corpus counts, not as 40 independent statistical replications, and no independence-based \(p\)-value, naive binomial inference, or unmodeled effective sample size is used.

Within the pre-specified 40-paper source scopes, the observed pattern is consequently \(40/40\) \(A0\), with no positive \(A1\) or \(A2\) case. This is uniform descriptive compatibility with direct composition under a pre-specified reconstruction contract faithful to each source model. It does not show that stateless mediation or persistent coordination are universally unnecessary, and the evidence remains insufficient for population-level or globally independent discrimination among those possibilities.

\section{System--environment boundary and experimental context}

Residual analysis exposed a further boundary problem: cognitive-system structure should not silently absorb environmental or experimental structure.

The final architecture separates
\[
\Gsys,
\qquad
\World,
\qquad
\Coupling,
\qquad
\Experiment,
\qquad
R.
\]

Here \(\Gsys\) is the structural role grammar, \(\World\) is an environmental carrier relative to the selected modeled-system boundary, \(\Coupling\) is cross-boundary coupling, \(\Experiment\) is experimental protocol, and \(R\) is a realized finite run/history.

Three non-identities are essential:
\[
K\neq\Gamma,
\qquad
C\neq\text{experimental condition},
\qquad
\Pin/\Pout\neq\text{experimental schedule}.
\]

Markov-blanket formalisms provide a stronger boundary construction based on conditional independence \citep{Friston2013Life,KirchhoffEtAl2018MarkovBlankets}. Work critical of unrestricted Markov-blanket generalization likewise emphasizes that formal boundary constructions do not by themselves settle which biological or cognitive system is scientifically appropriate \citep{RajaEtAl2021MarkovBlanketTrick}. The structural role grammar's \(\Pi\) is deliberately weaker: a Markov blanket may be a specialization when its additional probabilistic conditions are established, but it is not identified with \(\Pi\) by default.

The system--environment boundary remains application-relative. This separation is an architectural typing distinction, not a claim that cognition is environmentally isolated. Dynamical, embodied, enactive, or extended approaches may choose boundaries that include substantial body or environmental structure, and the coupling relation can remain constitutive for the modeled process \citep{FavelaRaja2026Dynamical}. The structural role grammar does not supply a universal scientific criterion for where every cognitive system must end; it requires the chosen boundary to be made explicit so that internal transformation, cross-boundary coupling, and experimental scheduling are not silently conflated.

\subsection{Experiment start as a run cut}

A finite experiment need not begin when the cognitive system begins. Its starting time \(t_0\) is treated as a cut through a potentially longer history:
\[
\cdots
\rightarrow
X_{t-2}
\rightarrow
X_{t-1}
\rightarrow
X_{t_0}
\rightarrow
X_{t_1}
\rightarrow\cdots.
\]

The intrinsic statement
\[
C_{t_0}(x_{t_0})
\]
only says that the realized state is admissible at the cut. It does not explain why that state was realized. Earlier system history, prior World coupling, retained structure, and experimental preparation may all contribute.

Thus
\[
\text{intrinsic admissibility}
\neq
\text{realized cut state}
\neq
\text{experimental preparation}.
\]

This leaves endogenous initialization and retention as open downstream questions rather than forcing an initialization primitive into the structural role grammar.

\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Construct validity and cognitive ontologies}

the present study is downstream of classical construct-validity problems rather than a substitute for them \citep{CronbachMeehl1955ConstructValidity,BorsboomMellenberghVanHeerden2004Validity}. Cognitive Atlas explicitly represents mental concepts and their relations to experimental tasks \citep{PoldrackEtAl2011CognitiveAtlas}, while the Cognitive Paradigm Ontology formalizes experimental paradigms in terms of stimuli, instructions, and responses \citep{TurnerLaird2012CogPO}. Philosophical work also stresses that cognitive ontology itself has multiple senses and that the relevant taxonomy depends on the explanatory and scientific role at issue \citep{JanssenKleinSlors2017CognitiveOntology}. Broader informatics work frames the search for mental structure as a problem of revising and relating cognitive categories \citep{PoldrackYarkoni2016CognitiveOntologies}. These projects are complementary precedents: they organize concepts and tasks, whereas the present study holds source-grounded claim structures fixed and tests structural relations under a declared projection.

\subsection{Cognitive individuation, mechanisms, and invariance}

The present method is positioned before, rather than instead of, stronger theories of cognitive individuation. Francken, Slors, and Craver distinguish interlocking operationalization, abstraction, and boundary problems in attempts to map capacities to mechanisms \citep{FranckenSlorsCraver2022CognitiveOntology}. Wajnerman-Paz and Rojas-Líbano further argue that across-context mechanism/capacity identity requires an invariance criterion linking behavioral profiles and mechanisms \citep{WajnermanPazRojasLibano2026Bidimensional}. the present study does not replace either project. Its narrower role is to ask what structure is available for such individuation once vocabulary is denied constitutive authority and the preservation criterion is made explicit.

Natural-kind reasoning raises a related warning about boundaries and interests. Onishi and Serpico argue, in the context of homeostatic property-cluster accounts, that category delineation by causal structure can remain interest-relative rather than yielding a ready-made taxonomy independently of inquiry purposes \citep{OnishiSerpico2022HPC}. The system--environment and experiment distinctions here force boundary choices into the open, but they do not eliminate interest-relativity or discover a unique natural taxonomy.

\subsection{Representation choice and comparative relevance}

North argues that representational choice can matter to scientific significance even when multiple representations are available \citep{North2026Perspicuous}. the present study makes a weaker claim: it does not rank its original factorization over TAIF by an intrinsic notion of perspicuity, but it treats factorization as scientifically consequential because it determines which preserved distinctions are first-class and which are indexed.

Brousalis emphasizes that scientific theory comparison depends on identifying similarities and differences relevant to the epistemic purpose of the comparison \citep{Brousalis2026TheoryComparison}. The explicit preservation contract here plays an analogous methodological role: it declares in advance which differences count for structural identity and prevents an apparent match from being detached from the criterion under which it was obtained.

\subsection{Dynamics and POMDP-like systems}

The \(X/K/T\) backbone has clear precedent in dynamical approaches to cognition \citep{VanGelder1998DynamicalHypothesis}. POMDPs likewise provide a canonical partially observable decision-system organization \citep{KaelblingLittmanCassandra1998POMDP}. the present study claims no novelty for these components. Its comparator question is whether the distinctions recovered from the corpus survive as first-class structure under coarser views.

\subsection{Category-theoretic and compositional formalisms}

Category theory has already been used to explain structural systematicity in cognition \citep{PhillipsWilson2010CategorialCompositionality}. Categorical cybernetics and Bayesian-lens-style formalisms provide prior mathematical machinery for bidirectional open systems \citep{StClereSmithe2021CyberKittens}. These are plausible realization languages for parts of the structural role grammar, not evidential sources from which the role inventory was derived. More broadly, none of the individual roles in the structural role grammar is claimed as a novel generic system primitive; the contribution concerns their corpus-grounded recovery, preservation relations, residual behavior, and losses under coarser projections.


\subsection{A recent structural-realist neighbor in JGPS}

A particularly close recent methodological neighbor is Beni's proposal for consciousness science \citep{Beni2026KindsWithoutStructure}. Beni argues that natural-kind reasoning should be supplemented by a structural-realist framework and uses predictive processing as a candidate formal basis for identifying invariant relations across competing theories. The present project shares the methodological emphasis on structure over surface labels, but it is not itself a natural-kind theory or a replacement structural realism; its inferential route is different. It does not select predictive processing, or any other cognitive theory, as the unifying framework in advance. Instead it begins from a source-audited heterogeneous claim corpus, suppresses construct labels, holds the comparison rules fixed, preserves the complete failure of the whole-claim atlas, and only then performs a secondary bounded reconstruction from which the structural role grammar is recovered and attacked by residual and comparator tests. The contrast is therefore between a philosophically motivated candidate unifying structure and an empirically reconstructed role grammar under a fixed audit protocol; the two projects are complementary rather than mutually exclusive.

\section{Limitations and Validation Boundary}

The first limitation is extraction reliability. The 60-claim corpus is source-audited, but independent extractor agreement is not established. The current evidence supports auditability of the retained reference corpus, not autonomous reproducibility of extraction.

Second, both empirical surfaces are designed rather than probability samples. The original 60 claims cannot estimate the prevalence of roles or structures across cognitive science, and the prospective 40-paper corpus cannot estimate the prevalence of \(A0\), \(A1\), or \(A2\) outcomes in the literature.

Third, the basis and forgetting operator are representation choices. TAIF demonstrates that the exact first-class inventory is non-unique under an invertible refactorization. CPCG adds a genuinely lossy sensitivity test: 206 objects become 143 signatures and 63/99 cross-stratum families collide, while 62 cross-stratum signatures and cross-stratum support for 58/60 claims remain. This is stronger evidence that the reuse result is not an artifact of one exact coding, but it is still evidence from only one lossy rival and does not establish representation neutrality.

Fourth, the prospective discriminative closure controls are conditional on the direct-preservation criterion. Their value is that none of six weaker bases reproduces 40/40 A0: each yields 40/40 A1 because an explicitly preserved distinction must be re-exposed. Because the all-eight-role usage pattern was already visible in the frozen prospective matrix before this control was specified, the test is a deterministic non-vacuity check rather than a blinded replication. It constrains one triviality objection without establishing absolute minimality, generic non-encodability, or a uniquely privileged role inventory.

Fifth, the system grammar is not a complete scientific claim language. Statements about evidence, model rejection, non-entailment, latent inference, comparison, and scientific uncertainty may require a separate assertion layer even when the underlying system roles are sufficient.

Sixth, the Lean formalization verifies declared typing and dependency structure, relational transition/observation signatures, carry semantics, and the explicit forgetful comparator maps. Its scientific contribution is therefore audit and constraint: it makes some illicit identifications and hidden assumptions mechanically visible. It is not an empirical validation layer and does not prove empirical adequacy, unique semantics, or cognitive truth.

Seventh, the prospective 40-paper corpus is exact-DOI-disjoint from the original 60-claim corpus, but it is not an independent random holdout. The roster was purposively fixed for architectural coverage, and the genealogy audit did not certify pairwise independence. Exact DOI disjointness therefore supports source-identity separation from the original analysis surface but does not license binomial significance, independence-based population inference, or a claim that the 40 papers are independent discoveries.

Eighth, \(A0\) is a structural-fidelity result under mechanisms specified by the source model. It is not a numerical replay of every model, a matched predictive-performance benchmark, or a proof that the source mechanism is empirically correct. Conversely, zero observed \(A1\) and \(A2\) cases does not show that stateless adapters or additional stateful coordinators are impossible in other architectures. The INT-10 and INT-16 real-source-derived controls show that the classifier can leave \(A0\) after declared load-bearing ablations, but these counterfactuals are not new empirical corpus observations.

Finally, independent human re-adjudication was not performed. Procedural auditability is established; inter-rater reliability remains unmeasured. The prospective 40-paper corpus result therefore remains a single-adjudicator result rather than an inter-rater reliability study. Language-model checks, deterministic CI, formal verification, and the post hoc matched calibration test different properties and are not substitutes for an independent human coder. The supplementary paper-level evidence table exposes the exact analysis stream version, outcome artifact, source evidence ledger, unresolved evidence, and genealogy limitation for all 40 papers so that external re-adjudication is possible without relying on the aggregate label. Population-scale replication across fresh source families and a universal criterion for selecting the cognitive-system boundary also remain unestablished.

\section{Discussion}

The main philosophical result is an evidential-burden principle for pre-individuation comparison. A capacity-individuation claim is evidentially incomplete when the relevant evidence consists only of inherited terminology or of successful reconstruction inside an undeclared or untested representation. To use claim-level structural identity or difference as evidence about capacities, one must state at least the comparison representation, the preservation criterion, and the granularity at which identity is asserted. Where representation sensitivity is material, plausible rivals must also be tested. This target is a form of inference, not a claim that any particular cited author commits the error in its strongest form.

The claim/capacity distinction is essential. This study directly compares scientific claims about capacities. It does not infer that two capacities are identical whenever two claim fragments match, nor that capacities are distinct whenever complete claims are incomparable. Instead, claim-level comparison supplies a constraint on what can count as evidence in a later individuation argument. That is why the contribution is pre-individuative rather than a rival cognitive ontology.

The empirical sequence shows why all three parts of the burden matter. Granularity matters because the whole-claim surface yields 1,770/1,770 incomparable pairs, whereas the separately declared bounded surface yields 206 reusable objects and 99 cross-stratum families. Vocabulary is insufficient in both directions: ATT01 and BLF01 carry different inherited labels but share a bounded object, while MEM04 and CNC05 both carry the label \emph{semantic memory} yet have disjoint bounded structural supports, remaining disjoint even after CPCG projection. A label therefore cannot serve as the preservation criterion it is supposed to justify.

Representation choice matters for a second reason. TAIF shows constructively that the nine first-class roles are not unique: five tagged families can preserve the same information. CPCG then removes information rather than merely repackaging it. The exact object inventory changes substantially---206 objects become 143 signatures and 63 of 99 cross-stratum families collide---yet 62 cross-stratum signatures and cross-stratum support for 58 of 60 claims survive. The appropriate conclusion is neither that the original grammar is uniquely true nor that everything is representation arbitrary. Rather, some higher-order reuse is stable across the tested representations while finer individuation depends on what the comparison preserves.

The prospective result addresses a different failure mode. Under the full basis, all 40 separately rostered source identities remain A0, including all 16 integrated papers. That result could have been trivial if substantially weaker bases also closed every case at A0 simply by retaining source mechanisms. The new closure controls show that they do not under the same direct-preservation least-lift rule: the dynamic backbone, the POMDP-like view, and four single-role ablations each produce 0 A0 and 40 A1. The full-basis 40/40 result is therefore discriminative relative to the declared preservation contract rather than an automatic consequence of allowing source-defined complexity.

This finding must be read with its exact scope. The weaker bases can in many cases encode the same source models in a broader mathematical sense. The A1 classifications say that, once distinctions already declared preservation-bearing are removed from first-class representation, stateless structure must be added to re-expose them. They are not claims that dynamical systems or POMDPs are scientifically inadequate, and they are not new empirical outcomes for the 40 source papers. The control tests whether the paper's own A0 notion is absorbing under weak representations; on the frozen structures, it is not.

The original prospective A-state result also remains distinct from the real-source perturbation controls. INT-10 moves from its certified A0 state to an A1 counterfactual when a load-bearing source-defined interface is hidden; INT-16 moves to A2 when history-bearing traces are hidden while delayed eligibility remains required. These controls show that realistic source-derived structures can leave A0 under the least-lift rule, but they are constructed damage tests rather than prevalence evidence.

The analysis sharpens the relation to existing philosophical work. Francken, Slors, and Craver's operationalization, abstraction, and boundary problems identify precisely where a capacity-to-mechanism mapping can become unstable; the present procedure supplies an auditable comparison condition before such mappings are promoted to identity claims. Wajnerman-Paz and Rojas-Líbano make invariance central to mechanism/capacity individuation; here, preservation criteria and rival-representation tests specify what must be held invariant for a particular comparison. Onishi and Serpico's interest-relativity warning remains in force because choosing a preservation criterion and system boundary is itself an inquiry-relative methodological act rather than a discovery of a ready-made taxonomy.

North and Brousalis are relevant at the more general level of representation and comparison. The present result agrees that representational choice and epistemic purpose can change which similarities matter, but it turns that point into a procedural burden: the choice must be declared and its consequences exposed. Relative to Beni's structural-realist proposal, the contribution is deliberately prior and weaker. It does not select a unifying structural theory of cognition; it asks what evidential discipline a structural-unification proposal should satisfy before successful unification is treated as capacity-level identity evidence.

The failure/recovery sequence also changes how negative results should be read. Universal whole-claim incomparability is not global incommensurability and is not erased by the later bounded analysis. It states that the chosen whole-claim preservation contract is too discriminating for the intended reuse question. Bounded reconstruction then asks a new question at a new granularity. Keeping both results visible prevents the retrospective move in which a successful lower-resolution representation is treated as though it had validated the original higher-resolution identity claim.

The resulting evidential trajectory is:
\[
\begin{aligned}
1770/1770\ \text{whole-claim incomparable}
&\rightarrow 206\ \text{bounded objects}
\rightarrow 99\ \text{cross-stratum families}\\
&\rightarrow 0/17\ \text{residuals requiring a new top-level role}
\rightarrow 40/40\ \text{prospective A0}\\
&\rightarrow \text{weaker-basis closure: }0/40\ \text{A0}
\rightarrow \text{lossy-rival survival: }62\ \text{cross-stratum signatures}.
\end{aligned}
\]

This sequence supports a specific philosophical proposition, not an ontology: structural comparison can discipline capacity individuation only when the evidential level, representation, preservation criterion, and granularity are explicit. The method thereby converts familiar warnings about labels and representation dependence into inspectable obligations whose failure and sensitivity can be measured.

The result remains corpus-relative and author-mediated. The 60-claim and 40-paper surfaces are purposive rather than probability samples; genealogy is unresolved globally; only one genuinely lossy rival has been tested; and the extraction/adjudication pipeline lacks an independent human second coder. Procedural auditability is established; inter-rater reliability remains unmeasured. These limitations bound the evidential claim rather than disappearing behind the deterministic audits.

\section{Conclusion}

This paper establishes a bounded pre-individuation proposition: \emph{Construct-based individuation is evidentially incomplete unless the comparison representation, preservation criterion, and granularity are independently stated; neither terminology nor successful reconstruction licenses representation-independent identity.} The direct evidence concerns structured scientific claims about cognitive capacities. Its role is to constrain later capacity-level individuation, not to settle that ontology directly.

The empirical argument has five linked parts. First, whole-claim comparison preserved all source distinctions strongly enough that all 1,770 pairs were incomparable. Second, a separately declared bounded reconstruction recovered 206 reusable structural objects and 99 cross-stratum families. Third, reverse projection into the fixed role grammar yielded 21 full, 22 partial, and 17 residual cases, with 0/17 residuals establishing a new top-level role. Fourth, the separately fixed 40-paper prospective corpus yielded
\[
A0=40,\qquad A1=0,\qquad A2=0,
\]
including 24/24 component and 16/16 integrated papers at A0. Fifth, the new discriminative closure control shows that none of six weaker tested bases inherits this result: the dynamic backbone, POMDP-like view, and four single-role ablations each yield 0 A0 and 40 A1 under the same direct-preservation least-lift rule.

Representation sensitivity is likewise two-sided. Lossless TAIF demonstrates that the exact nine-role first-class inventory is non-unique. Genuinely lossy CPCG then compresses 206 bounded objects to 143 signatures and collapses 63/99 cross-stratum families, yet retains 62 cross-stratum signatures and cross-stratum support for 58/60 claims. Together with the ATT01--BLF01 different-label/shared-structure witness and the MEM04--CNC05 same-label/different-structure witness, this shows that vocabulary and structural preservation can diverge in both directions.

The A-state controls remain bounded as well. Counterfactual damage to the real source-derived INT-10 structure moves it from A0 to A1 by requiring a stateless typed bridge; damage to INT-16 moves it to A2 by requiring history-bearing persistent state. These do not alter the certified prospective 40-paper outcomes and do not license prevalence inference.

The paper therefore does not establish a universal natural-kind theory, a representation-neutral ontology, absolute minimality, generic non-encodability of dynamical or POMDP formalisms, a universal system boundary, or 40 independent replications. It instead supplies an auditable evidential burden for using claim-level structural comparison in capacity individuation. Independent human re-adjudication remains unperformed. Procedural auditability is established; inter-rater reliability remains unmeasured.
