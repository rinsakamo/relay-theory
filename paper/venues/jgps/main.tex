\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} cognitive ontology; structural comparison; cognitive capacities; construct labels; partial observability; philosophy of cognitive science


\begin{abstract}

Scientific theories of cognition divide mental capacities into constructs such as memory, learning, skill, attention, prediction, control, belief, and concept. Those labels organize research but do not by themselves establish structural individuation. This paper tests a bounded philosophical proposition: cognitive-capacity labels are at most prima facie guides to individuation, while claims of structural identity or difference require an explicit preservation criterion under a declared comparison representation. A designed stress-test corpus of 60 source-grounded claims was compared under a pre-specified construct-label-neutral structural representation. Whole-claim comparison left all 1,770 pairs incomparable. A secondary bounded reconstruction recovered 206 unique reusable structural subobjects and 99 cross-stratum families; reverse projection yielded a recurrent nine-role grammar with no top-level role gap. A separately pre-specified, single-adjudicator prospective compatibility test examined 40 cognitive-model papers with zero exact DOI overlap with the original corpus. All 40 were reconstructed at A0: 24/24 component-model papers and 16/16 integrated-model papers required neither a reconstruction-added stateless adapter nor an additional persistent coordinator. A frozen rival factorization preserved the higher-order comparison results while changing which distinctions were first-class, and two real-source-derived counterfactual perturbations reached A1 and A2 without altering the certified 40-paper outcomes. The prospective result remains corpus-relative: genealogy was not certified independent, the sample was purposive, and independent human re-adjudication was not performed. The contribution is therefore a comparison/pre-individuation procedure, not a natural-kind theory, prevalence result, universal cognitive architecture, or representation-neutral ontology.

\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Cognitive science inherits a large vocabulary of capacities: memory, attention, learning, belief, skill, prediction, control, concepts, and many others. These labels are useful for communication and literature organization, but they create a methodological risk. Different names can suggest distinct capacities before structural evidence for that distinction has been established, while one familiar name can cover heterogeneous mechanisms, measurements, timescales, and experimental operations.

The construct-validity tradition already rejects the idea that a psychological construct validates itself by being named \citep{CronbachMeehl1955ConstructValidity}. Stronger causal accounts of validity likewise emphasize that a measurement claim requires more than terminological continuity \citep{BorsboomMellenberghVanHeerden2004Validity}. Cognitive-ontology projects make the problem computationally explicit by representing mental concepts and their links to experimental tasks \citep{PoldrackEtAl2011CognitiveAtlas,TurnerLaird2012CogPO,PoldrackYarkoni2016CognitiveOntologies}. The present paper asks a narrower question: can scientific claims about cognitive capacities be compared on a common structural surface without allowing historical construct names or source authority to determine the result?

The top-level philosophical proposition tested here is deliberately bounded: \emph{Cognitive-capacity labels are at most prima facie guides to individuation. Claims of structural identity or difference require an explicit preservation criterion under a declared comparison representation.} This is not a natural-kind theory, a universal ontology, or a representation-neutral criterion of individuation.

Three philosophical theses organize the analysis. First, construct individuation is not inherited from vocabulary: historical labels may guide case selection and communication, but they do not settle structural identity. Second, whole-claim incomparability can be an informative negative-compression result rather than evidence of global incommensurability: failure at one comparison granularity can motivate a separately declared bounded-reuse question without rewriting the failed result. Third, successful reconstruction is representation-relative: a representation can support stable comparative judgments under an explicit preservation contract without thereby being metaphysically privileged, uniquely minimal, or invariant under every alternative factorization.

Accordingly, the contribution is best understood as a comparison or pre-individuation procedure. It asks whether inherited construct labels track reusable structure under an explicit preservation criterion before stronger natural-kind, mechanistic, or invariance-based individuation claims are made.

The target is not a replacement dictionary of cognition. It is an auditable sequence:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{source claim}
&\rightarrow
\text{label-suppressed structural representation}
\rightarrow
\text{comparison}
\\
&\rightarrow
\text{bounded reusable structure}
\rightarrow
\text{system-role grammar}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The setting is cognition under partial observability and bounded information. Scientific papers rarely expose a complete cognitive system. They provide restricted observations, interventions, behavioral outcomes, neural measurements, model variables, task manipulations, and relations among them. A common structural comparison surface must therefore compare partial structures without pretending that every source reveals a complete mechanism.

The procedure is construct-label-neutral only in a declared, limited sense. It suppresses inherited construct labels and selected source-authority metadata when comparing source-grounded structures; it does not claim neutrality among theories, ontologies, or representation languages. The analysis deliberately adopts a system-theoretic structural representation and asks what survives within that declared representation.

Two safeguards govern the analysis. First, author identity, institution, venue, citation count, and historical construct label are excluded from the structural comparison logic. Second, structural similarity is never promoted to semantic or ontological identity.

The methodological novelty claim is intentionally narrow. Generic dynamics, POMDP components, boundary formalisms, lenses, category theory, and the individual roles in the final grammar all have substantial prior art. The candidate contribution here is the integration of source-grounded label suppression, pre-specified structural comparison, preservation of failed compression attempts, bounded subobject reconstruction, reverse-projection testing, residual adjudication, rival-factorization sensitivity, and real-source-derived counterfactual controls into one empirical reconstruction protocol. Historical firstness for that integration is not claimed.

The broader philosophy-of-science issue is not specific to cognition. Scientific vocabularies routinely inherit categories whose names organize inquiry before their structural relations are well understood. The methodological question is therefore how to distinguish genuine structure from distinctions carried mainly by terminology, source convention, or representational choice. Paper 2 treats cognitive-capacity labels as one demanding test case for that more general problem of scientific representation, individuation, and structural comparison.

\section{Methods}

\subsection{Designed corpus}

The bounded empirical target contains 60 claims. Forty-eight primary claims are divided evenly across eight historical construct strata. Twelve additional claims form a non-tuning challenge surface.

The primary strata are Memory, Learning, Skill, Attention, Prediction, Control, Belief, and Concept. Challenge pressures include multi-timescale organization, stochastic or approximate structure, constitutive boundary pressure, compositional or systematic structure, embodied or world-coupled organization, normative or criterion structure, hybrid labels, label-coordinate mismatch, multi-condition structure, same-inventory/different-topology pressure, strict-refinement pressure, and high structural heterogeneity. Challenge claims were not permitted to tune the structured-record schema, working basis, or comparison semantics.

This is a literature-based structural comparison, not an experiment or prevalence study. Its role is instead adversarial and structural: the 48 primary claims provide balanced coverage across historically familiar construct strata, while the 12 challenge claims probe predeclared pressures that could break the representation. Consequently, the supported inference is whether reusable structure survives this designed stress-test surface under pre-specified rules, not how prevalent any role or reusable structural subobject is in cognitive science as a whole.

\subsection{Source-grounded structured claim records}

Each selected source claim was represented in a machine-readable structured claim record containing stable source identity, source-local evidence locators, claim scope and modality, typed nodes, typed relations, temporal information, control information, approximation state, and bridge assumptions.

A language model assisted initial structured extraction, but its output was not treated as authority. Every retained record was reviewed against source text, and all 60 current records carry reviewed status. The scientific input to downstream analysis is therefore the source-audited structured claim corpus.

Independent extraction reliability was not established. Historical local two-pass calibration transactions produced zero valid structured claim record records in both the v2 and v3 real calibration runs, leaving no valid A/B agreement denominator. Paper 2 consequently claims auditability of the retained corpus, not inter-model agreement, autonomous extraction reliability, or deterministic language-model replay.

\subsection{Pre-specification chronology and anti-circularity}

The analysis was separated into pre-specified stages to limit feedback from later results into earlier representations. Source-grounded structured claim records were reviewed before reconstruction with the structural role grammar. The working basis \(B_{P2}\), the whole-claim projection \(\Phi\), structural adjudications, and the reusable-subobject identities were then held fixed before the 60-claim reverse projection into the structural role grammar. The reverse-projection analysis streams used those reviewed, fixed structures and did not reread source papers or rewrite the structured claim records to improve grammar coverage. Residuals were preserved and classified rather than repaired by adding roles after the fact.

This stage separation does not establish the absence of interpretive judgment: source review and structural adjudication remain author-mediated. Its purpose is narrower. It prevents the final Grammar from serving simultaneously as the rule used to construct the source representation on which its own apparent adequacy is evaluated.

\subsection{Use of generative AI and human accountability}

OpenAI ChatGPT was used as a structured research assistant for initial structured claim extraction, source-analysis and audit workflow assistance in the prospective composition study, code and workflow drafting, and manuscript drafting and editing. Model output was not treated as source authority or as an independent adjudicator. Every retained structured claim record record in the original 60-claim corpus was reviewed by the author against the corresponding source text before entering the fixed reference corpus. The prospective composition study separately used evidence tied to explicit source locations, versioned staged artifacts, and conservative integration checks; language-model output did not override source evidence or versioned adverse findings. Downstream quantitative results are defined by versioned machine-readable artifacts and deterministic audits. The author reviewed the final manuscript and assumes full responsibility for the accuracy, interpretation, and presentation of the work.

\subsection{Pre-specified structural basis}

The structural comparison suppresses source information that should not determine similarity merely by appearing in a paper. Author identity, institution, venue, citation count, historical construct label, source-local identifiers, and free-text presentation are excluded from comparison.

The pre-specified working basis is
\[
B_{P2}=\{S,\Pi,K,O,T,C,Q,P\},
\]
interpreted as a declared structural coordinate system for comparison rather than an ontology or a representation-neutral basis. Lexical shortcuts are forbidden: a source node named \emph{condition} is not automatically \(C\); a reward-like term does not exhaust \(Q\); temporal precedence is not automatically \(K\); and a partition need not be physical.

\subsection{Whole-claim comparison}

The pre-specified \(\Phi\) projection preserves claim type, modality, scope shape, typed topology, active basis coordinates, temporal presence states, control states, approximation mode, and bridge-grounding multiplicity. It forgets source authority and presentation metadata.

Pairwise relations distinguish equivalence, refinement, forgetting, incomparability, and mutual-embed non-isomorphism. For 60 claims, the unordered comparison surface contains
\[
\binom{60}{2}=1770
\]
pairs.

\subsection{Secondary bounded reconstruction}

Because whole-claim comparison failed universally, the next analysis changed the \emph{unit of reuse}, not the pre-specified source representation. The whole-claim result therefore remains the primary result for the original \(\Phi\)-level question. A secondary post-atlas reconstruction then asked a different question: whether bounded substructures could recur even when complete scientific claims remained incomparable. In that chronological sense the reconstruction is post-atlas; it is not presented as if it had been the successful primary comparison from the outset.

The bounded reconstruction searched for basis-subobjects recoverable under a pre-specified forgetting operator. The operator permits restriction to retained typed substructure but forbids arbitrary rewiring and semantic relabeling. Thus the secondary analysis does not revise the 1,770 whole-claim verdicts, and successful bounded recurrence cannot be used to retrospectively relabel a failed whole-claim pair as equivalent.

Negative and ablation controls tested trivial-core collapse, label dependence, permissive forgetting, richer competing cores, overlapping incomparable cores, cross-label recurrence, compatibility with whole-claim incomparability, and presentation invariance. No post-outcome abstraction threshold was added to manufacture recurrence.

\subsection{Structural role grammar and adversarial tests}

Surviving bounded structures motivated a compact structural role grammar (version 0):
\[
\Gsys=\{\Pi,X,C,Q,\Pin,\Pout,K,T,\rho/O\}.
\]
Here \(X\) is a generic configuration carrier; \(C\) is realizability/admissibility structure; \(Q\) is criterion-conditioned orientation; \(\Pin\) and \(\Pout\) are directional interface roles; \(K\) is typed transformation or dependence; \(T\) is succession or temporal indexing; and \(\rho/O\) is boundary-relative observation or trace.

A mnemonic representation is
\[
X_{n+1}=K_{n;C,Q,\Pin}(X_n),
\qquad
O_n=\rho_{\Pi,C,\Pout,n}(X_n),
\]
without assuming that every claim instantiates every role.

All 60 reviewed claims were reverse-projected into this structural role grammar without rereading source papers or changing the structured claim records, \(B_{P2}\), \(\Phi\), structural adjudications, or reusable-subobject identities. Residuals were classified under a pre-specified taxonomy in which only an unexplained requirement for an additional top-level system role counted as prima facie evidence for extending the role inventory.

Two strict distinction-preservation comparators were tested:
\[
G_{\mathrm{dyn}}=\{X,K,T\},
\qquad
G_{\mathrm{pomdp}}=\{X,P,K,\rho/O,Q,T\}.
\]
These are distinction-loss tests, not non-encodability theorems.


\subsection{Prospective 40-paper compatibility test}

After the structural role grammar and the original 60-claim analyses were held fixed, a separate prospective mechanism-composition study was authorized under a new outcome-blind source roster. Its pre-specified denominator contained 40 cognitive-model papers: 24 component-oriented papers, with three assigned to each of eight sampling strata, and 16 integrated papers chosen to expose cross-mechanism composition. The 40 exact DOIs have zero overlap with the original 60-claim source manifest. This source-identity separation does not imply genealogical independence, because model lineages and shared constituents can cross DOI boundaries.

This was a single-adjudicator prospective compatibility test under a pre-specified reconstruction contract, not an inter-rater reliability study. Independent human re-adjudication was not performed. Language-model assistance, deterministic CI, formal verification, and later synthetic calibration are not counted as independent human adjudication.

The repository chronology makes the prospective boundary inspectable without requiring repository-internal milestone names in the manuscript. The protocol restricting adjudication to source-supported evidence, the genealogy criterion, the bounded genealogy audit, and the explicit prospective-study authorization were committed before the six scientific analysis streams produced their final outcomes. That authorization fixed the exact 40-paper denominator, no-substitution rule, structural role grammar, adopted method reference, and A0/A1/A2 classification boundary. Full commit identifiers and timestamps are provided in the supplementary provenance ledger. The earlier roster artifact retains its historical working/outcome-blind designation; later authorization fixed that exact roster as the no-substitution denominator rather than retroactively rewriting its earlier state.

Each prospective paper followed a source-first staged audit before reconstruction: source decomposition, a second audit informed by the first-pass result using fixed prior artifacts, objection adjudication restricted to source-supported evidence, and only then reconstruction with the fixed structural role grammar and architectural adjudication. Per-paper architectural outcomes were attempted in a predeclared order. \(A0\) denotes direct composition faithful to the source model using the pre-specified grammar roles together with mechanisms already defined by the source. \(A1\) is reached only if \(A0\) fails and fidelity requires a reconstruction-added typed but stateless interface, sequencing, or data-translation contract. \(A2\) is reached only if both \(A0\) and \(A1\) fail and fidelity requires an additional load-bearing persistent stateful mechanism that is not reducible to mechanisms specified by the source model, pre-specified grammar roles, or an already modeled control function.

Equivalently, let \(\mathcal R_0\hookrightarrow\mathcal R_1\hookrightarrow\mathcal R_2\) be nested reconstruction classes permitting, respectively, no reconstruction-added mechanism, stateless reconstruction-added mediation, and persistent/stateful reconstruction-added mediation. If \(E_i\) denotes the source contracts admitting a faithful lift from \(\mathcal R_i\), then
\[
A0=E_0,\qquad
A1=E_1\setminus E_0,\qquad
A2=E_2\setminus E_1.
\]
This minimal-faithful-reconstruction formulation makes the classes mutually exclusive under the declared nesting. Eligibility and evidential sufficiency are tested first; ineligible sources are classified as source ineligible, while unresolved source evidence is classified as underdetermined. If no faithful reconstruction exists even after the allowed A2 extension and that non-reconstruction is supported by the source evidence rather than merely unresolved, the result is classified as a localized failure.

Hierarchy, gating, belief or memory state, planner/search state, learned parameters, recurrence, accumulators, temporal ordering, routing metadata, and environment state were therefore not automatically counted as \(A2\). A difficult fit or localized failure was likewise not sufficient evidence for \(A2\). No paper could be replaced because of an unfavorable reconstruction result.

The 40 papers were analyzed in six isolated analysis streams and combined only after exact version ancestry, versioned outcome artifacts, and conservative integration checks were verified. At the study level, three possibilities were distinguished: direct composition without reconstruction-added coordination-specific structure; recurring reconstruction-added stateless mediation; and recurring reconstruction-added persistent coordination. Per-paper \(A0/A1/A2\) categories provide evidence about these possibilities rather than automatic population-level verdicts.

A separate genealogy audit was retained as an inferential constraint. Its purpose was not to remove inconvenient papers after reconstruction but to prevent the 40 papers from being treated as statistically independent replications when shared ancestry remained unresolved.

\section{Results}

\subsection{Whole-claim comparison fails}
\label{sec:wholefail}

The pre-specified whole-claim result was:
\[
1770/1770=\text{incomparable}.
\]

Across 3,540 directed comparisons, 3,518 failed at the scalar stage, six reached node-count comparison, 16 reached node-label multiset comparison, and none reached topology or embedding. Universal incomparability was therefore localized primarily to the enriched pre-topology comparison surface rather than to graph topology itself.

This negative result is retained as evidence rather than removed from the final narrative.

\subsection{Bounded reusable structure reappears}

The secondary bounded reconstruction produced 213 within-stratum candidate subobjects and 206 unique reusable structural subobjects. Seven exact cross-stratum equivalence components survived. The global refinement structure contains 470 direct refinement edges and 2,894 strict subobject relations in transitive closure.

The bounded family structure contains
\[
99\ \text{cross-stratum families}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
107\ \text{within-stratum-only families},
\]
with 188 distinct support signatures. Fifty-nine of 60 claims are covered by at least one surviving bounded object. All eight negative and ablation controls pass.

Notably, 1,304 whole-claim pairs share at least one surviving reusable structural subobject despite every whole-claim pair being held as incomparable. Whole-claim identity and reusable substructure are therefore distinct questions on the same corpus.

Generic dynamical cognition is established prior art \citep{VanGelder1998DynamicalHypothesis}, and contemporary dynamical cognitive science explicitly emphasizes temporally extended brain--body--environment organization \citep{FavelaRaja2026Dynamical}. The result here is not that dynamical models are invalid. Likewise, canonical POMDPs provide state, action, transition, observation, and reward/criterion organization for partially observable control \citep{KaelblingLittmanCassandra1998POMDP}.

These comparators are not fitted predictive models, performance benchmarks, or statistical competitors. No reward optimization, prior/posterior tuning, predictive-accuracy comparison, or parameter fitting is performed. The test is strictly representational: under the declared forgetful projection, which distinctions remain first-class and which become implicit or disappear? The result is therefore only that the tested natural projections omit distinctions explicitly preserved by the pre-specified corpus; it does not show that enriched dynamical or POMDP formalisms are incapable of encoding them. In that limited sense, the reason for not adopting the POMDP-like view as the comparison basis is not insufficient generic encoding power but excessive forgetting relative to distinctions from the source models that this study had held fixed as first-class.

\subsection{Rival-basis sensitivity: a lossless tagged factorization}

The Dynamic and POMDP-like comparators deliberately forget distinctions and therefore do not answer whether the paper's higher-order conclusions depend on the exact nine-role factorization. A separate sensitivity analysis was frozen before evaluation. The rival Tagged Admissibility--Interface Factorization (TAIF) reorganizes the same frozen information into five first-class families:
\[
G_{\mathrm{TAIF}}=\{X,A,I,K,T\}.
\]
The \(A\) family carries indexed subtypes for partition/boundary information, realizability constraints, and criterion orientation; the \(I\) family carries indexed subtypes for inward write/intervention, outward probe/query, and observation/trace. Thus \(\Pi,C,Q\) become indexed \(A\) subtypes and \(\Pin,\Pout,\rho/O\) become indexed \(I\) subtypes, while \(X,K,T\) remain first-class. The subtype indices are preservation-bearing structure, and the map is explicitly invertible.

The deterministic audit round-tripped all nine Grammar-v0 role atoms, all eight \(B_{P2}\) axes, and all 60 frozen reverse-projection role sets. The tagged recoding remained injective for all 206 bounded structural objects, while the frozen support relation still contained 99 cross-stratum families. The whole-claim result remained 1,770/1,770 incomparable under lossless decoding, and the different-label ATT01/BLF01 witness continued to share bounded object \(A\)-3b814de4da04.

This is a sensitivity result rather than a second ontology. The exact nine-role inventory is not representation-invariant: TAIF makes admissibility and interface families first-class while moving several distinctions into indexed subtypes. What survives is the higher-order claim that comparison requires an explicit preservation criterion, that whole-claim failure and bounded reuse remain distinct questions, and that different inherited labels can share reusable structure.

\subsection{Post hoc discriminability stress test of the A0/A1/A2 classification rule}

An adversarial calibration performed after the prospective 40-paper analysis tested whether the A0/A1/A2 classification rule itself was structurally attracted to A0. This calibration is explicitly post hoc and does not alter or re-adjudicate any prospective paper.

The A0/A1/A2 rule was expressed in terms of the minimal faithful reconstruction level within the nested reconstruction classes above and tested on two matched synthetic families: a strict dynamical family and a POMDP-like family. Each family contains one direct case, one typed stateless-bridge case, and one history-sensitive persistent-bridge case. The six expected outcomes are therefore two A0, two A1, and two A2 cases, and all six classes were reached as constructed.

The A1 witness uses incompatible declared port types: direct composition fails, while an explicit stateless map repairs the mismatch. The A2 witness requires the bridge output to depend on the immediately preceding binary input. No current-input-only function can satisfy both histories that share the same present input but require different outputs; a one-bit persistent bridge can. The corresponding Lean formalization verifies the minimal-reconstruction partition, the positive A1 witness, the stateless obstruction for A2, the stateful A2 realization, and the factorization of structural role grammar forgetting through the POMDP-like view to the strict dynamical transition view.

This calibration establishes reachability of A1 and A2 under the pre-specified reconstruction-addition semantics. It does not establish independent-human adjudication reliability, empirical frequency of the three outcomes, or non-encodability of the tested structures in richer dynamical or POMDP formalisms.

The remaining permitted paper-level final states were also zero: localized failure, underdetermination, and source ineligibility. No recurring reconstruction-added stateless interface class and no reconstruction-added persistent/stateful coordinator survived the adversarial checks.

The integrated arm is especially informative for the composition question. Its 16 papers contain mixtures, gating, hierarchy, planning, accumulation, feedback, recurrent state, and neuromodulatory coupling specified by the source models, yet all 16 remained \(A0\). The result therefore does not say that stateful coordination is absent from cognitive models. It says that, for these source models, faithfully preserving the coordination already present in the source did not require inventing an additional coordination layer during reconstruction.

The within-corpus genealogy audit covered all
\[
\binom{40}{2}=780
\]
unordered pairs. Of these, 670 remained underdetermined, 85 had a bounded difference between source models, 25 shared a constituent, and none was certified as a direct or explicitly shared whole-model ancestry pair. Crucially, absence of a discovered path, citation, or common constituent was never promoted to genealogical independence. The 40 outcomes are therefore reported as raw corpus counts, not as 40 independent statistical replications, and no independence-based \(p\)-value, naive binomial inference, or unmodeled effective sample size is used.

Within the pre-specified 40-paper source scopes, the observed pattern is consequently \(40/40\) \(A0\), with no positive \(A1\) or \(A2\) case. This is uniform descriptive compatibility with direct composition under a pre-specified reconstruction contract faithful to each source model. It does not show that stateless mediation or persistent coordination are universally unnecessary, and the evidence remains insufficient for population-level or globally independent discrimination among those possibilities.

\section{System--environment boundary and experimental context}

Residual analysis exposed a further boundary problem: cognitive-system structure should not silently absorb environmental or experimental structure.

The final architecture separates
\[
\Gsys,
\qquad
\World,
\qquad
\Coupling,
\qquad
\Experiment,
\qquad
R.
\]

Here \(\Gsys\) is the structural role grammar, \(\World\) is an environmental carrier relative to the selected modeled-system boundary, \(\Coupling\) is cross-boundary coupling, \(\Experiment\) is experimental protocol, and \(R\) is a realized finite run/history.

Three non-identities are essential:
\[
K\neq\Gamma,
\qquad
C\neq\text{experimental condition},
\qquad
\Pin/\Pout\neq\text{experimental schedule}.
\]

Markov-blanket formalisms provide a stronger boundary construction based on conditional independence \citep{Friston2013Life,KirchhoffEtAl2018MarkovBlankets}. Work critical of unrestricted Markov-blanket generalization likewise emphasizes that formal boundary constructions do not by themselves settle which biological or cognitive system is scientifically appropriate \citep{RajaEtAl2021MarkovBlanketTrick}. The structural role grammar's \(\Pi\) is deliberately weaker: a Markov blanket may be a specialization when its additional probabilistic conditions are established, but it is not identified with \(\Pi\) by default.

The system--environment boundary remains application-relative. This separation is an architectural typing distinction, not a claim that cognition is environmentally isolated. Dynamical, embodied, enactive, or extended approaches may choose boundaries that include substantial body or environmental structure, and the coupling relation can remain constitutive for the modeled process \citep{FavelaRaja2026Dynamical}. The structural role grammar does not supply a universal scientific criterion for where every cognitive system must end; it requires the chosen boundary to be made explicit so that internal transformation, cross-boundary coupling, and experimental scheduling are not silently conflated.

\subsection{Experiment start as a run cut}

A finite experiment need not begin when the cognitive system begins. Its starting time \(t_0\) is treated as a cut through a potentially longer history:
\[
\cdots
\rightarrow
X_{t-2}
\rightarrow
X_{t-1}
\rightarrow
X_{t_0}
\rightarrow
X_{t_1}
\rightarrow\cdots.
\]

The intrinsic statement
\[
C_{t_0}(x_{t_0})
\]
only says that the realized state is admissible at the cut. It does not explain why that state was realized. Earlier system history, prior World coupling, retained structure, and experimental preparation may all contribute.

Thus
\[
\text{intrinsic admissibility}
\neq
\text{realized cut state}
\neq
\text{experimental preparation}.
\]

This leaves endogenous initialization and retention as open downstream questions rather than forcing an initialization primitive into the structural role grammar.

\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Construct validity and cognitive ontologies}

Paper 2 is downstream of classical construct-validity problems rather than a substitute for them \citep{CronbachMeehl1955ConstructValidity,BorsboomMellenberghVanHeerden2004Validity}. Cognitive Atlas explicitly represents mental concepts and their relations to experimental tasks \citep{PoldrackEtAl2011CognitiveAtlas}, while the Cognitive Paradigm Ontology formalizes experimental paradigms in terms of stimuli, instructions, and responses \citep{TurnerLaird2012CogPO}. Philosophical work also stresses that cognitive ontology itself has multiple senses and that the relevant taxonomy depends on the explanatory and scientific role at issue \citep{JanssenKleinSlors2017CognitiveOntology}. Broader informatics work frames the search for mental structure as a problem of revising and relating cognitive categories \citep{PoldrackYarkoni2016CognitiveOntologies}. These projects are complementary precedents: they organize concepts and tasks, whereas Paper 2 holds source-grounded claim structures fixed and tests structural relations under a declared projection.

\subsection{Cognitive individuation, mechanisms, and invariance}

The present method is positioned before, rather than instead of, stronger theories of cognitive individuation. Francken, Slors, and Craver distinguish interlocking operationalization, abstraction, and boundary problems in attempts to map capacities to mechanisms \citep{FranckenSlorsCraver2022CognitiveOntology}. Wajnerman-Paz and Rojas-Líbano further argue that across-context mechanism/capacity identity requires an invariance criterion linking behavioral profiles and mechanisms \citep{WajnermanPazRojasLibano2026Bidimensional}. Paper 2 does not replace either project. Its narrower role is to ask what structure is available for such individuation once vocabulary is denied constitutive authority and the preservation criterion is made explicit.

Natural-kind reasoning raises a related warning about boundaries and interests. Onishi and Serpico argue, in the context of homeostatic property-cluster accounts, that category delineation by causal structure can remain interest-relative rather than yielding a ready-made taxonomy independently of inquiry purposes \citep{OnishiSerpico2022HPC}. The system--environment and experiment distinctions here force boundary choices into the open, but they do not eliminate interest-relativity or discover a unique natural taxonomy.

\subsection{Representation choice and comparative relevance}

North argues that representational choice can matter to scientific significance even when multiple representations are available \citep{North2026Perspicuous}. Paper 2 makes a weaker claim: it does not rank its original factorization over TAIF by an intrinsic notion of perspicuity, but it treats factorization as scientifically consequential because it determines which preserved distinctions are first-class and which are indexed.

Brousalis emphasizes that scientific theory comparison depends on identifying similarities and differences relevant to the epistemic purpose of the comparison \citep{Brousalis2026TheoryComparison}. The explicit preservation contract here plays an analogous methodological role: it declares in advance which differences count for structural identity and prevents an apparent match from being detached from the criterion under which it was obtained.

\subsection{Dynamics and POMDP-like systems}

The \(X/K/T\) backbone has clear precedent in dynamical approaches to cognition \citep{VanGelder1998DynamicalHypothesis}. POMDPs likewise provide a canonical partially observable decision-system organization \citep{KaelblingLittmanCassandra1998POMDP}. Paper 2 claims no novelty for these components. Its comparator question is whether the distinctions recovered from the corpus survive as first-class structure under coarser views.

\subsection{Category-theoretic and compositional formalisms}

Category theory has already been used to explain structural systematicity in cognition \citep{PhillipsWilson2010CategorialCompositionality}. Categorical cybernetics and Bayesian-lens-style formalisms provide prior mathematical machinery for bidirectional open systems \citep{StClereSmithe2021CyberKittens}. These are plausible realization languages for parts of the structural role grammar, not evidential sources from which the role inventory was derived. More broadly, none of the individual roles in the structural role grammar is claimed as a novel generic system primitive; the contribution concerns their corpus-grounded recovery, preservation relations, residual behavior, and losses under coarser projections.


\subsection{A recent structural-realist neighbor in JGPS}

A particularly close recent methodological neighbor is Beni's proposal for consciousness science \citep{Beni2026KindsWithoutStructure}. Beni argues that natural-kind reasoning should be supplemented by a structural-realist framework and uses predictive processing as a candidate formal basis for identifying invariant relations across competing theories. The present project shares the methodological emphasis on structure over surface labels, but it is not itself a natural-kind theory or a replacement structural realism; its inferential route is different. It does not select predictive processing, or any other cognitive theory, as the unifying framework in advance. Instead it begins from a source-audited heterogeneous claim corpus, suppresses construct labels, holds the comparison rules fixed, preserves the complete failure of the whole-claim atlas, and only then performs a secondary bounded reconstruction from which the structural role grammar is recovered and attacked by residual and comparator tests. The contrast is therefore between a philosophically motivated candidate unifying structure and an empirically reconstructed role grammar under a fixed audit protocol; the two projects are complementary rather than mutually exclusive.

\section{Limitations and Validation Boundary}

The first limitation is extraction reliability. The 60-claim corpus is source-audited, but independent extractor agreement is not established. The current evidence supports auditability of the retained reference corpus, not autonomous reproducibility of extraction.

Second, both empirical surfaces are designed rather than probability samples. The original 60 claims cannot estimate the prevalence of roles or structures across cognitive science, and the prospective 40-paper corpus cannot estimate the prevalence of \(A0\), \(A1\), or \(A2\) outcomes in the literature.

Third, the basis and forgetting operator are representation choices. The TAIF sensitivity analysis shows that the higher-order conclusions survive at least one lossless alternative factorization while the set of first-class roles changes. That is positive sensitivity evidence, not representation neutrality: many other bases and forgetting operators remain untested, and the current choices are not established as uniquely optimal.

Fourth, the granularity of the structural role grammar is non-unique. TAIF compresses the nine first-class roles into five tagged families without losing the frozen distinctions, demonstrating the non-uniqueness constructively. The strict distinction-preservation result therefore establishes nontriviality of the preserved distinctions relative to the fixed corpora, not absolute mathematical minimality or a uniquely privileged role inventory.

Fifth, the system grammar is not a complete scientific claim language. Statements about evidence, model rejection, non-entailment, latent inference, comparison, and scientific uncertainty may require a separate assertion layer even when the underlying system roles are sufficient.

Sixth, the Lean formalization verifies declared typing and dependency structure, relational transition/observation signatures, carry semantics, and the explicit forgetful comparator maps. Its scientific contribution is therefore audit and constraint: it makes some illicit identifications and hidden assumptions mechanically visible. It is not an empirical validation layer and does not prove empirical adequacy, unique semantics, or cognitive truth.

Seventh, the prospective 40-paper corpus is exact-DOI-disjoint from the original 60-claim corpus, but it is not an independent random holdout. The roster was purposively fixed for architectural coverage, and the genealogy audit did not certify pairwise independence. Exact DOI disjointness therefore supports source-identity separation from the original analysis surface but does not license binomial significance, independence-based population inference, or a claim that the 40 papers are independent discoveries.

Eighth, \(A0\) is a structural-fidelity result under mechanisms specified by the source model. It is not a numerical replay of every model, a matched predictive-performance benchmark, or a proof that the source mechanism is empirically correct. Conversely, zero observed \(A1\) and \(A2\) cases does not show that stateless adapters or additional stateful coordinators are impossible in other architectures. The INT-10 and INT-16 real-source-derived controls show that the classifier can leave \(A0\) after declared load-bearing ablations, but these counterfactuals are not new empirical corpus observations.

Finally, independent human re-adjudication was not performed. Procedural auditability is established; inter-rater reliability remains unmeasured. The prospective 40-paper corpus result therefore remains a single-adjudicator result rather than an inter-rater reliability study. Language-model checks, deterministic CI, formal verification, and the post hoc matched calibration test different properties and are not substitutes for an independent human coder. The supplementary paper-level evidence table exposes the exact analysis stream version, outcome artifact, source evidence ledger, unresolved evidence, and genealogy limitation for all 40 papers so that external re-adjudication is possible without relying on the aggregate label. Population-scale replication across fresh source families and a universal criterion for selecting the cognitive-system boundary also remain unestablished.

\section{Discussion}

The philosophical proposition supported by the full analysis is narrower than a cognitive ontology: cognitive-capacity labels are at most prima facie guides to individuation, and claims of structural identity or difference require an explicit preservation criterion under a declared comparison representation. The data do not establish that the chosen representation is uniquely correct; the TAIF sensitivity result instead shows directly that first-class factorization can change while the higher-level comparative conclusions survive.

The main empirical pattern begins with a failure/recovery sequence. At the whole-claim level, the pre-specified comparison surface was too discriminating to reveal reusable structure: all 1,770 pairs were incomparable. Preserving that result demonstrates that precise formalization alone is not enough; a comparison surface can be internally coherent yet scientifically vacuous for an intended compression question.

The secondary bounded reconstruction changes the question from \emph{are these complete scientific claims the same?} to \emph{do these claims contain recoverable reusable substructure under a pre-specified forgetting rule?} Under that question, cross-label recurrence appears without deleting the whole-claim failure. The reusable structural subobject result is therefore not a repaired primary atlas. It is a different, explicitly motivated level of analysis.

The structural role grammar reconstruction then asks whether surviving structures can be expressed through a compact recurring role inventory. The answer is positive on the bounded corpus, but it is not a universal ontology. Role counts show broad recurrence, residual analysis fails to establish an additional top-level role, and comparator tests show that coarser natural projections forget distinctions. Together these results support a structural comparison grammar under a declared preservation contract.

The single-adjudicator prospective 40-paper corpus compatibility test adds a distinct test: whether the pre-specified structural role grammar can reconstruct mechanism composition specified by the source models outside the original 60-claim source identities without adding a new coordination layer. The exact DOI overlap between the two corpora is zero. Under the predeclared \(A0\rightarrow A1\rightarrow A2\) order, all 40 prospective papers remained \(A0\), including all 16 integrated papers. No reconstruction-added stateless adapter and no reconstruction-added persistent coordinator was required.

This result should not be read as evidence that cognitive systems contain no stateful coordination. Many tested sources explicitly contain gating, hierarchy, workspace-like state, learned state, planners, accumulators, recurrent dynamics, or other coordinating mechanisms. The methodological result is narrower: when those mechanisms are retained as structure specified by the source model, the reconstruction did not need to posit an additional generic coordinator merely to make heterogeneous mechanisms compose. Complexity by itself did not force \(A2\).

The study-level interpretation is therefore asymmetric. The pre-specified 40-paper corpus is uniformly descriptively compatible with direct composition under a reconstruction contract faithful to each source model, while supplying no positive case requiring added stateless mediation or added persistent coordination. But the purposive roster and unresolved genealogy prevent the \(40/40\) count from becoming a population-frequency estimate or a universal hypothesis verdict. Exact DOI disjointness from the original 60 claims strengthens the prospective separation of the two analysis surfaces; it does not establish lineage independence.

The contribution of the structural role grammar still does not lie in the mathematical novelty of its individual roles. State or configuration, transition, observation, constraints, interfaces, and temporal structure all have extensive prior art. The result concerns which distinctions survived the fixed reconstruction procedure, which distinctions disappeared under coarser projections, and whether either residual claims or a separate composition corpus required an additional top-level role.

The structural role grammar is not proposed as a demarcation criterion for cognition. A thermostat, chemical system, or other non-cognitive system may be describable with analogous generic roles; that fact is not by itself a counterexample. The scientific question here is not which structure uniquely defines cognition, but which structural distinctions recur across heterogeneous scientific claims about cognition under the pre-specified comparison protocol.

The evidential trajectory can now be stated as
\[
\begin{aligned}
1770/1770\ \text{whole-claim incomparable}
&\rightarrow 206\ \text{reusable structural subobjects}
\\
&\rightarrow 99\ \text{cross-stratum families}
\\
&\rightarrow \text{60-claim reverse projection}
\\
&\rightarrow 0/17\ \text{residuals requiring a new top-level role}
\\
&\rightarrow 40/40\ \text{prospective }A0\text{ composition}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The strict distinction-preservation comparator results and the prospective composition result constrain different failure modes. The former ask what distinctions coarser projections lose; the latter asks whether a separately pre-specified set of mechanisms specified by the source models requires reconstruction-added coordination. Their conjunction is stronger than either result alone, while remaining explicitly corpus-relative.

The system--environment separation further limits interpretation. A cognitive mechanism should not be defined by absorbing experimental preparation or environmental state into internal structure whenever a claim mentions a condition. System grammar, claim language, source context, formal realization, environment, coupling, and experiment therefore remain distinct architectural levels.

The broader methodological claim can be stated compactly:
\[
\text{different labels}\not\Rightarrow\text{different structures},
\qquad
\text{same labels}\not\Rightarrow\text{same structures}.
\]
A structural distinction must survive the declared construct-label-neutral structural audit to become evidence within this comparison framework.

Three philosophical consequences follow from that evidential rule. First, construct individuation is not inherited from vocabulary: a construct label is a historical or communicative handle, whereas structural identity in the present framework is warranted only by preservation under the declared comparison map. Second, universal whole-claim incomparability is not evidence that the scientific claims are meaningless or globally incommensurable. It shows that the chosen whole-claim granularity preserved too many distinctions to support the intended reusable-structure comparison, which is itself an informative negative compression result. Third, successful reconstruction is representation-relative. The structural role grammar earns support as a useful comparison basis under the pre-specified preservation contract; that success does not by itself establish metaphysical realism about the roles, a uniquely correct ontology, or invariance under every admissible representation.

The TAIF control sharpens this point: the original nine-role grammar and a five-family tagged recoding preserve the same frozen distinctions while assigning first-class status differently. The supported philosophical claim is therefore about preservation under an explicit representation, not about metaphysical privilege for one role inventory.

This also locates the method relative to mechanistic, natural-kind, and invariance-based individuation. Those approaches ask which capacities or mechanisms genuinely belong together. Paper 2 supplies a prior comparison discipline: before a vocabulary-defined grouping is used as evidence for individuation, source claims can be tested for preserved structural commonality under a declared criterion.

For general philosophy of science, the point is methodological rather than taxonomic. Scientific categories can be treated as hypotheses about reusable structure rather than as primitives whose individuation is inherited from vocabulary. Negative compression results remain informative under this view, while successful prospective composition can test whether a recovered grammar merely summarizes its construction corpus or continues to organize newly fixed source identities. The same strategy can in principle be applied whenever mature scientific vocabularies mix historical labels, heterogeneous operationalizations, and partially observed systems, although such transfer remains to be tested rather than assumed.

\section{Conclusion}

The central philosophical result is the following bounded pre-individuation claim: \emph{Cognitive-capacity labels are at most prima facie guides to individuation. Claims of structural identity or difference require an explicit preservation criterion under a declared comparison representation.}

This paper develops a bounded construct-label-neutral structural comparison basis for cognitive-capacity claims. Sixty source-grounded claims were represented under a common contract, compared without granting construct labels structural authority, and subjected to a preserved sequence of failed and successful compression tests. The neutrality claim is deliberately limited: the procedure suppresses inherited construct labels and selected source-authority metadata while adopting a declared system-theoretic structural representation.

Whole-claim comparison failed universally:
\[
1770/1770\ \text{pairs were incomparable}.
\]
A secondary bounded-subobject reconstruction then recovered 206 unique reusable structural subobjects, including 99 cross-stratum families. Reverse projection of the fixed 60 claims into the structural role grammar yielded 21 full, 22 partial, and 17 residual cases. Residual adjudication established that none of the 17 residual claims required an additional top-level system role.

A separately pre-specified single-adjudicator prospective compatibility corpus then tested 40 cognitive-model papers, using mechanisms specified by the sources, with zero exact DOI overlap with the original 60-claim source manifest. The predeclared composition order yielded
\[
A0=40,\qquad A1=0,\qquad A2=0,
\]
with component papers \(24/24\) \(A0\) and integrated papers \(16/16\) \(A0\). No reconstruction-added stateless interface class or additional persistent/stateful coordinator was required. A later post hoc matched Dynamic/POMDP-like calibration established that A1 and A2 are reachable under the same reconstruction-addition semantics, without re-adjudicating any paper in the prospective corpus. This is a source-scoped structural result, not 40 independent replications: the genealogy audit did not certify independence, independent human re-adjudication was not performed, and no population-frequency or binomial inference is licensed.

The recurrent system-role grammar remains
\[
\boxed{\{\Pi,X,C,Q,\Pin,\Pout,K,T,\rho/O\}}.
\]

A frozen rival-factorization sensitivity test recoded the same distinctions into five first-class tagged families, \(\{X,A,I,K,T\}\), with exact round-trip preservation across the nine role atoms, the 60 reverse-projection records, and all 206 bounded objects. The higher-order conclusions survived, but the identity of the first-class role inventory did not.

Two separate real-source-derived counterfactual controls also left \(A0\): an INT-10 interface ablation required an \(A1\) stateless typed bridge, and an INT-16 history-state ablation required an \(A2\) persistent bridge. Neither control alters the certified prospective result \(A0=40,A1=0,A2=0\).

The combined result does not establish nine ontological primitives, a complete language of cognitive science, a full measurement theory, absolute mathematical minimality, representative population coverage, a universal direct-composition claim, independent extraction reliability, a universal system boundary, or a demarcation criterion for cognition. Its contribution is instead a reproducible failure-aware and prospectively extended reconstruction:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{heterogeneous cognitive claims}
&\rightarrow
\text{construct-label-neutral decomposition}
\\
&\rightarrow
\text{bounded reusable structure}
\rightarrow
\text{shared system-role grammar}
\\
&\rightarrow
\text{prospective compatibility: }40/40\ A0.
\end{aligned}
\]

The next scientific tests are fresh outcome-blind source waves and dynamic questions about retention and reuse, rather than further tuning on either fixed corpus. Independent human re-adjudication remains a distinct external validation opportunity, not a result claimed by the present single-author study. Procedural auditability is established; inter-rater reliability remains unmeasured.
