\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} cognitive ontology; structural comparison; cognitive capacities; construct labels; partial observability; philosophy of cognitive science


\begin{abstract}
Scientific theories of cognition divide mental capacities into constructs such as memory, learning, skill, attention, prediction, control, belief, and concept. Those labels organize research but do not by themselves establish structural individuation. This paper develops a construct-label-neutral structural comparison procedure for cognitive-capacity claims under partial observability and bounded information. \emph{Construct-label-neutral} is deliberately narrower than theory-, ontology-, or representation-neutral: the comparison suppresses inherited construct labels and selected source-authority metadata while adopting a declared system-theoretic structural representation. A designed stress-test corpus of 60 claims---48 primary claims across eight construct strata and 12 non-tuning challenge claims---was converted into source-grounded ClaimIR representations and reviewed against source text. Whole-claim comparison produced a preserved negative result: all 1,770 claim pairs were incomparable under the frozen rich comparison surface. A secondary bounded reconstruction recovered 206 unique global Archetype/XLike objects and 99 cross-lane families. Reverse projection supports a recurrent role grammar comprising partition, configuration, realizability constraints, criterion-conditioned orientation, directional interfaces, typed transformation or dependence, temporal succession, and boundary-relative observation; residual adjudication found no top-level role gap. Coarser dynamical and POMDP-like projections lose frozen distinctions under strict preservation tests. The result is a bounded, source-audited structural comparison basis, not a full measurement theory, a demarcation criterion for cognition, or a representation-neutral ontology.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Cognitive science inherits a large vocabulary of capacities: memory, attention, learning, belief, skill, prediction, control, concepts, and many others. These labels are useful for communication and literature organization, but they create a methodological risk. Different names can suggest distinct capacities before structural evidence for that distinction has been established, while one familiar name can cover heterogeneous mechanisms, measurements, timescales, and experimental operations.

The construct-validity tradition already rejects the idea that a psychological construct validates itself by being named \citep{CronbachMeehl1955ConstructValidity}. Stronger causal accounts of validity likewise emphasize that a measurement claim requires more than terminological continuity \citep{BorsboomMellenberghVanHeerden2004Validity}. Cognitive-ontology projects make the problem computationally explicit by representing mental concepts and their links to experimental tasks \citep{PoldrackEtAl2011CognitiveAtlas,TurnerLaird2012CogPO,PoldrackYarkoni2016CognitiveOntologies}. The present paper asks a narrower question: can scientific claims about cognitive capacities be compared on a common structural surface without allowing historical construct names or source authority to determine the result?

The target is not a replacement dictionary of cognition. It is an auditable sequence:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{source claim}
&\rightarrow
\text{label-suppressed structural representation}
\rightarrow
\text{comparison}
\\
&\rightarrow
\text{bounded reusable structure}
\rightarrow
\text{system-role grammar}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The setting is cognition under partial observability and bounded information. Scientific papers rarely expose a complete cognitive system. They provide restricted observations, interventions, behavioral outcomes, neural measurements, model variables, task manipulations, and relations among them. A common structural comparison surface must therefore compare partial structures without pretending that every source reveals a complete mechanism.

The procedure is construct-label-neutral only in a declared, limited sense. It suppresses inherited construct labels and selected source-authority metadata when comparing source-grounded structures; it does not claim neutrality among theories, ontologies, or representation languages. The analysis deliberately adopts a system-theoretic structural representation and asks what survives within that declared representation.

Two safeguards govern the analysis. First, author identity, institution, venue, citation count, and historical construct label are excluded from the structural comparison logic. Second, structural similarity is never promoted to semantic or ontological identity.

The methodological novelty claim is intentionally narrow. Generic dynamics, POMDP components, boundary formalisms, lenses, category theory, and the individual roles in the final grammar all have substantial prior art. The candidate contribution here is the integration of source-grounded label suppression, frozen structural comparison, preservation of failed compression attempts, bounded subobject reconstruction, reverse-projection testing, residual adjudication, and strict-preservation comparators into one empirical reconstruction protocol. Historical firstness for that integration is not claimed.

The broader philosophy-of-science issue is not specific to cognition. Scientific vocabularies routinely inherit categories whose names organize inquiry before their structural relations are well understood. The methodological question is therefore how to distinguish genuine structure from distinctions carried mainly by terminology, source convention, or representational choice. Paper 2 treats cognitive-capacity labels as one demanding test case for that more general problem of scientific representation, individuation, and structural comparison.

\section{Methods}

\subsection{Designed corpus}

The bounded empirical target contains 60 claims. Forty-eight primary claims are divided evenly across eight historical construct strata. Twelve additional claims form a non-tuning challenge surface.

\centering

\label{tab:corpus}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}

Challenge & 12 predeclared pressure classes & 12 \\
\midrule
Total & & 60 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

The primary strata are Memory, Learning, Skill, Attention, Prediction, Control, Belief, and Concept. Challenge pressures include multi-timescale organization, stochastic or approximate structure, constitutive boundary pressure, compositional or systematic structure, embodied or world-coupled organization, normative or criterion structure, hybrid labels, label-coordinate mismatch, multi-condition structure, same-inventory/different-topology pressure, strict-refinement pressure, and high structural heterogeneity. Challenge claims were not permitted to tune the ClaimIR schema, working basis, or comparison semantics.

The sample size is not justified as a statistical estimator of a literature-wide frequency. Its role is instead adversarial and structural: the 48 primary claims provide balanced coverage across historically familiar construct strata, while the 12 challenge claims probe predeclared pressures that could break the representation. Consequently, the supported inference is whether reusable structure survives this designed stress-test surface under frozen rules, not how prevalent any role or Archetype is in cognitive science as a whole.

\subsection{Source-grounded ClaimIR}

Each selected source claim was represented in a machine-readable ClaimIR containing stable source identity, source-local evidence locators, claim scope and modality, typed nodes, typed relations, temporal information, control information, approximation state, and bridge assumptions.

A language model assisted initial structured extraction, but its output was not treated as authority. Every retained record was reviewed against source text, and all 60 current records carry reviewed status. The scientific input to downstream analysis is therefore the source-audited ClaimIR corpus.

Independent extraction reliability was not established. Historical local two-pass calibration transactions produced zero valid ClaimIR records in both the v2 and v3 real calibration runs, leaving no valid A/B agreement denominator. Paper 2 consequently claims auditability of the retained corpus, not inter-model agreement, autonomous extraction reliability, or deterministic language-model replay.

\subsection{Freeze chronology and anti-circularity}

The analysis was separated into frozen stages to limit feedback from later results into earlier representations. Source-grounded ClaimIR records were reviewed before the Grammar-v0 reconstruction. The working basis \(B_{P2}\), the whole-claim projection \(\Phi\), structural adjudications, and the bounded Archetype identities were then frozen before the 60-claim reverse projection into Grammar v0. The reverse-projection lanes used those reviewed frozen structures and did not reread source papers or rewrite ClaimIR to improve Grammar coverage. Residuals were preserved and classified rather than repaired by adding roles after the fact.

This stage separation does not establish the absence of interpretive judgment: source review and structural adjudication remain author-mediated. Its purpose is narrower. It prevents the final Grammar from serving simultaneously as the rule used to construct the source representation on which its own apparent adequacy is evaluated.

\subsection{Use of generative AI and human accountability}

OpenAI ChatGPT was used as a structured research assistant for initial ClaimIR extraction, code and workflow drafting, and manuscript drafting and editing. Model output was not treated as source authority or as an independent adjudicator. Every retained ClaimIR record was reviewed by the author against the corresponding source text before entering the frozen reference corpus, and downstream quantitative results are defined by versioned machine-readable artifacts and deterministic audits. The author reviewed the final manuscript and assumes full responsibility for the accuracy, interpretation, and presentation of the work.

\subsection{Frozen structural basis}

The structural comparison suppresses source information that should not determine similarity merely by appearing in a paper. Author identity, institution, venue, citation count, historical construct label, source-local identifiers, and free-text presentation are excluded from comparison.

The frozen working basis is
\[
B_{P2}=\{S,\Pi,K,O,T,C,Q,P\},
\]
interpreted as a declared structural coordinate system for comparison rather than an ontology or a representation-neutral basis. Lexical shortcuts are forbidden: a source node named \emph{condition} is not automatically \(C\); a reward-like term does not exhaust \(Q\); temporal precedence is not automatically \(K\); and a partition need not be physical.

\subsection{Whole-claim comparison}

The frozen \(\Phi\) projection preserves claim type, modality, scope shape, typed topology, active basis coordinates, temporal presence states, control states, approximation mode, and bridge-grounding multiplicity. It forgets source authority and presentation metadata.

Pairwise relations distinguish equivalence, refinement, forgetting, incomparability, and mutual-embed non-isomorphism. For 60 claims, the unordered comparison surface contains
\[
\binom{60}{2}=1770
\]
pairs.

\subsection{Secondary bounded reconstruction}

Because whole-claim comparison failed universally, the next analysis changed the \emph{unit of reuse}, not the frozen source representation. The whole-claim result therefore remains the primary result for the original \(\Phi\)-level question. A secondary post-atlas reconstruction then asked a different question: whether bounded substructures could recur even when complete scientific claims remained incomparable. In that chronological sense the reconstruction is post-atlas; it is not presented as if it had been the successful primary comparison from the outset.

The bounded reconstruction searched for basis-subobjects recoverable under a frozen forgetting operator. The operator permits restriction to retained typed substructure but forbids arbitrary rewiring and semantic relabeling. Thus the secondary analysis does not revise the 1,770 whole-claim verdicts, and successful bounded recurrence cannot be used to retrospectively relabel a failed whole-claim pair as equivalent.

Destructive controls tested trivial-core collapse, label dependence, permissive forgetting, richer competing cores, overlapping incomparable cores, cross-label recurrence, compatibility with whole-claim incomparability, and presentation invariance. No post-outcome abstraction threshold was added to manufacture recurrence.

\subsection{Grammar v0 and hostile tests}

Surviving bounded structures motivated a compact role reinterpretation:
\[
\Gsys=\{\Pi,X,C,Q,\Pin,\Pout,K,T,\rho/O\}.
\]
Here \(X\) is a generic configuration carrier; \(C\) is realizability/admissibility structure; \(Q\) is criterion-conditioned orientation; \(\Pin\) and \(\Pout\) are directional interface roles; \(K\) is typed transformation or dependence; \(T\) is succession or temporal indexing; and \(\rho/O\) is boundary-relative observation or trace.

A mnemonic representation is
\[
X_{n+1}=K_{n;C,Q,\Pin}(X_n),
\qquad
O_n=\rho_{\Pi,C,\Pout,n}(X_n),
\]
without assuming that every claim instantiates every role.

All 60 frozen claims were reverse-projected into Grammar v0 without rereading source papers or changing ClaimIR, \(B_{P2}\), \(\Phi\), structural adjudications, or bounded Archetype identities. Residuals were classified under a frozen taxonomy in which only \texttt{ROLE\_GAP} counted as prima facie evidence for an additional top-level system role.

Two strict direct-preservation comparators were tested:
\[
G_{\mathrm{dyn}}=\{X,K,T\},
\qquad
G_{\mathrm{pomdp}}=\{X,P,K,\rho/O,Q,T\}.
\]
These are distinction-loss tests, not non-encodability theorems.


\section{Results}

\subsection{Whole-claim comparison fails}
\label{sec:wholefail}

The global frozen whole-claim result was:
\[
1770/1770=\text{INCOMPARABLE}.
\]

Across 3,540 directed comparisons, 3,518 failed at the scalar stage, six reached node-count comparison, 16 reached node-label multiset comparison, and none reached topology or embedding. Universal incomparability was therefore localized primarily to the enriched pre-topology comparison surface rather than to graph topology itself.

This negative result is retained as evidence rather than removed from the final narrative.

\subsection{Bounded reusable structure reappears}

The secondary bounded reconstruction produced 213 lane-local candidate objects and 206 unique global Archetype/XLike objects. Seven exact cross-lane equivalence components survived. The global refinement structure contains 470 direct refinement edges and 2,894 strict subobject relations in transitive closure.

The bounded family structure contains
\[
99\ \text{cross-lane families}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
107\ \text{lane-local-only families},
\]
with 188 distinct support signatures. Fifty-nine of 60 claims are covered by at least one surviving bounded object. All eight destructive controls pass.

Notably, 1,304 whole-claim pairs share at least one surviving Archetype despite every whole-claim pair being frozen as incomparable. Whole-claim identity and reusable substructure are therefore distinct questions on the same corpus.

Generic dynamical cognition is established prior art \citep{VanGelder1998DynamicalHypothesis}, and contemporary dynamical cognitive science explicitly emphasizes temporally extended brain--body--environment organization \citep{FavelaRaja2026Dynamical}. The result here is not that dynamical models are invalid. These comparators are not fitted predictive models, performance benchmarks, or statistical competitors. The test is strictly representational: under the declared forgetful projection, which distinctions remain first-class and which become implicit or disappear? The result is therefore only that the tested natural projections omit distinctions explicitly preserved by the frozen corpus; it does not show that enriched dynamical or POMDP formalisms are incapable of encoding them.

\section{System, World, Coupling, and Experiment}

Residual analysis exposed a further boundary problem: cognitive-system structure should not silently absorb environmental or experimental structure.

The final architecture separates
\[
\Gsys,
\qquad
\World,
\qquad
\Coupling,
\qquad
\Experiment,
\qquad
R.
\]

Here \(\Gsys\) is Grammar v0, \(\World\) is a World/environment carrier relative to the selected modeled-system boundary, \(\Coupling\) is cross-boundary coupling, \(\Experiment\) is experimental protocol, and \(R\) is a realized finite run/history.

Three non-identities are essential:
\[
K\neq\Gamma,
\qquad
C\neq\text{experimental condition},
\qquad
\Pin/\Pout\neq\text{experimental schedule}.
\]

Markov-blanket formalisms provide a stronger boundary construction based on conditional independence \citep{Friston2013Life,KirchhoffEtAl2018MarkovBlankets}. Work critical of unrestricted Markov-blanket generalization likewise emphasizes that formal boundary constructions do not by themselves settle which biological or cognitive system is scientifically appropriate \citep{RajaEtAl2021MarkovBlanketTrick}. Grammar-v0 \(\Pi\) is deliberately weaker: a Markov blanket may be a specialization when its additional probabilistic conditions are established, but it is not identified with \(\Pi\) by default.

The system/World boundary remains application-relative. This separation is an architectural typing distinction, not a claim that cognition is environmentally isolated. Dynamical, embodied, enactive, or extended approaches may choose boundaries that include substantial body or environmental structure, and the coupling relation can remain constitutive for the modeled process \citep{FavelaRaja2026Dynamical}. Grammar v0 does not supply a universal scientific criterion for where every cognitive system must end; it requires the chosen boundary to be made explicit so that internal transformation, cross-boundary coupling, and experimental scheduling are not silently conflated.

\subsection{Experiment start as a run cut}

A finite experiment need not begin when the cognitive system begins. Its starting time \(t_0\) is treated as a cut through a potentially longer history:
\[
\cdots
\rightarrow
X_{t-2}
\rightarrow
X_{t-1}
\rightarrow
X_{t_0}
\rightarrow
X_{t_1}
\rightarrow\cdots.
\]

The intrinsic statement
\[
C_{t_0}(x_{t_0})
\]
only says that the realized state is admissible at the cut. It does not explain why that state was realized. Earlier system history, prior World coupling, retained structure, and experimental preparation may all contribute.

Thus
\[
\text{intrinsic admissibility}
\neq
\text{realized cut state}
\neq
\text{experimental preparation}.
\]

This leaves endogenous initialization and retention as open downstream questions rather than forcing an initialization primitive into Grammar v0.

\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Construct validity and cognitive ontologies}

Paper 2 is downstream of classical construct-validity problems rather than a substitute for them \citep{CronbachMeehl1955ConstructValidity,BorsboomMellenberghVanHeerden2004Validity}. Cognitive Atlas explicitly represents mental concepts and their relations to experimental tasks \citep{PoldrackEtAl2011CognitiveAtlas}, while the Cognitive Paradigm Ontology formalizes experimental paradigms in terms of stimuli, instructions, and responses \citep{TurnerLaird2012CogPO}. Philosophical work also stresses that cognitive ontology itself has multiple senses and that the relevant taxonomy depends on the explanatory and scientific role at issue \citep{JanssenKleinSlors2017CognitiveOntology}. Broader informatics work frames the search for mental structure as a problem of revising and relating cognitive categories \citep{PoldrackYarkoni2016CognitiveOntologies}. These projects are complementary precedents: they organize concepts and tasks, whereas Paper 2 freezes source-grounded claim structures and tests structural relations under a declared projection.

\subsection{Dynamics and POMDP-like systems}

The \(X/K/T\) backbone has clear precedent in dynamical approaches to cognition \citep{VanGelder1998DynamicalHypothesis}. POMDPs likewise provide a canonical partially observable decision-system organization \citep{KaelblingLittmanCassandra1998POMDP}. Paper 2 claims no novelty for these components. Its comparator question is whether the distinctions recovered from the corpus survive as first-class structure under coarser views.

\subsection{Category-theoretic and compositional formalisms}

Category theory has already been used to explain structural systematicity in cognition \citep{PhillipsWilson2010CategorialCompositionality}. Categorical cybernetics and Bayesian-lens-style formalisms provide prior mathematical machinery for bidirectional open systems \citep{StClereSmithe2021CyberKittens}. These are plausible realization languages for parts of Grammar v0, not evidential sources from which the role inventory was derived. More broadly, none of the individual Grammar-v0 roles is claimed as a novel generic system primitive; the contribution concerns their corpus-grounded recovery, preservation relations, residual behavior, and losses under coarser projections.


\subsection{A recent structural-realist neighbor in JGPS}

A particularly close recent methodological neighbor is Beni's proposal for consciousness science \citep{Beni2026KindsWithoutStructure}. Beni argues that natural-kind reasoning should be supplemented by a structural-realist framework and uses predictive processing as a candidate formal basis for identifying invariant relations across competing theories. The present project shares the methodological emphasis on structure over surface labels, but its inferential route is different. It does not select predictive processing, or any other cognitive theory, as the unifying framework in advance. Instead it begins from a source-audited heterogeneous claim corpus, suppresses construct labels, freezes the comparison rules, preserves the complete failure of the whole-claim atlas, and only then performs a secondary bounded reconstruction from which Grammar v0 is recovered and attacked by residual and comparator tests. The contrast is therefore between a philosophically motivated candidate unifying structure and an empirically reconstructed role grammar under a fixed audit protocol; the two projects are complementary rather than mutually exclusive.

\section{Limitations and Validation Boundary}

The first limitation is extraction reliability. The 60-claim corpus is source-audited, but independent extractor agreement is not established. The current evidence supports auditability of the retained reference corpus, not autonomous reproducibility of extraction.

Second, the corpus is designed and small. It cannot estimate the prevalence of roles or structures across cognitive science. Population-scale validation remains downstream.

Third, the basis and forgetting operator are representation choices. The present controls show that reusable structure survives under the frozen choices; they do not establish that those choices are uniquely optimal. Alternative basis construction and alternative forgetting operators remain important future hostile tests.

Fourth, Grammar-v0 role granularity is non-unique. Equivalent factorizations may preserve the same distinctions. The strict-preservation result establishes nontriviality of the current inventory relative to the frozen corpus, not absolute mathematical minimality.

Fifth, the system grammar is not a complete scientific claim language. Statements about evidence, model rejection, non-entailment, latent inference, and comparison may require a separate assertion layer even when the underlying system roles are sufficient.

Sixth, the Lean formalization verifies declared typing and dependency structure, relational transition/observation signatures, carry semantics, and the explicit forgetful comparator maps. Its scientific contribution is therefore audit and constraint: it makes some illicit identifications and hidden assumptions mechanically visible. It is not an empirical validation layer and does not prove empirical adequacy, unique semantics, or cognitive truth.

Finally, the project has not yet tested prospective out-of-sample grammar recovery, independent human re-adjudication, or a universal criterion for selecting the cognitive-system boundary.

\section{Discussion}

The main empirical pattern is a failure/recovery sequence. At the whole-claim level, the frozen comparison surface was too discriminating to reveal reusable structure: all 1,770 pairs were incomparable. Preserving that result demonstrates that precise formalization alone is not enough; a comparison surface can be internally coherent yet scientifically vacuous for an intended compression question.

The secondary bounded reconstruction changes the question from \emph{are these complete scientific claims the same?} to \emph{do these claims contain recoverable reusable substructure under a frozen forgetting rule?} Under that question, cross-label recurrence appears without deleting the whole-claim failure. The Archetype result is therefore not a repaired primary atlas. It is a different, explicitly motivated level of analysis.

The Grammar-v0 reconstruction then asks whether surviving structures can be expressed through a compact recurring role inventory. The answer is positive on the bounded corpus, but it is not a universal ontology. Role counts show broad recurrence, residual analysis fails to establish an additional top-level role, and comparator tests show that coarser natural projections forget distinctions. Together these results support a structural comparison grammar under a declared preservation contract.

The contribution of Grammar v0 does not lie in the mathematical novelty of its individual roles. State or configuration, transition, observation, constraints, interfaces, and temporal structure all have extensive prior art. The result concerns which distinctions survived a frozen reconstruction of heterogeneous cognitive claims, which distinctions disappeared under coarser projections, and whether residual claims required an additional top-level role.

Grammar v0 is not proposed as a demarcation criterion for cognition. A thermostat, chemical system, or other non-cognitive system may be describable with analogous generic roles; that fact is not by itself a counterexample. The scientific question here is not which structure uniquely defines cognition, but which structural distinctions recur across heterogeneous scientific claims about cognition under the frozen comparison protocol.

The core methodological result is therefore the failure/recovery trajectory
\[
1770/1770\ \text{whole-claim incomparable}
\rightarrow
206\ \text{Archetypes}
\rightarrow
99\ \text{cross-lane families}
\rightarrow
\text{60-claim reverse projection}
\rightarrow
\mathrm{ROLE\_GAP}=0.
\]
The strict-preservation comparator results add a further constraint: coarser natural projections lose distinctions retained by the frozen corpus. This trajectory, rather than primitive-level mathematical novelty, is the evidential center of the proposal.

The system/World separation further limits interpretation. A cognitive mechanism should not be defined by absorbing experimental preparation or environmental state into internal structure whenever a claim mentions a condition. System grammar, claim language, source context, formal realization, World, coupling, and experiment therefore remain distinct architectural levels.

The broader methodological claim can be stated compactly:
\[
\text{different labels}\not\Rightarrow\text{different structures},
\qquad
\text{same labels}\not\Rightarrow\text{same structures}.
\]
A structural distinction must survive the declared construct-label-neutral structural audit to become evidence within this comparison framework.

For general philosophy of science, the point is methodological rather than taxonomic. Scientific categories can be treated as hypotheses about reusable structure rather than as primitives whose individuation is inherited from vocabulary. Negative compression results are informative under this view: the failed whole-claim atlas constrains what kind of unification is licensed, while the secondary bounded reconstruction identifies a weaker level at which structural recurrence survives. The same strategy can in principle be applied whenever mature scientific vocabularies mix historical labels, heterogeneous operationalizations, and partially observed systems, although such transfer remains to be tested rather than assumed.

\section{Conclusion}

This paper develops a bounded construct-label-neutral structural comparison basis for cognitive-capacity claims. Sixty source-grounded claims were represented under a common contract, compared without granting construct labels structural authority, and subjected to a preserved sequence of failed and successful compression tests. The neutrality claim is deliberately limited: the procedure suppresses inherited construct labels and selected source-authority metadata while adopting a declared system-theoretic structural representation.

Whole-claim comparison failed universally:
\[
1770/1770\ \text{pairs were incomparable}.
\]
A secondary bounded-subobject reconstruction then recovered 206 unique reusable Archetype/\allowbreak XLike objects, including 99 cross-lane families. Reverse projection of the frozen 60 claims into Grammar v0 yielded 21 FULL, 22 PARTIAL, and 17 RESIDUAL cases. Residual adjudication established no new top-level role gap:
\[
\mathrm{ROLE\_GAP}=0.
\]

The resulting recurrent system-role grammar is
\[
\boxed{\{\Pi,X,C,Q,\Pin,\Pout,K,T,\rho/O\}}.
\]

The result does not establish nine ontological primitives, a complete language of cognitive science, a full measurement theory, absolute mathematical minimality, representative population coverage, independent extraction reliability, a universal system boundary, or a demarcation criterion for cognition. Its contribution is instead a reproducible failure-aware reconstruction:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{heterogeneous cognitive claims}
&\rightarrow
\text{construct-label-neutral decomposition}
\\
&\rightarrow
\text{bounded reusable structure}
\rightarrow
\text{shared system-role grammar}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The next scientific test is prospective scale and dynamics rather than further tuning on the same 60 claims.
