# Cover letter draft — Foundations of Science

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript **“When May a Formal Difference Enter an Individuation Inference? Target Factorization, Restricted Tests, and Cross-Domain Audits”** as a Standard Article in *Foundations of Science*.

The manuscript develops a cross-disciplinary methodological framework for a foundational question that recurs across scientific representation, measurement, database curation, and evidence synthesis: when may a difference internal to a representation legitimately enter an inference to a difference in the represented target? Relative to a fixed proposed representation-to-target assignment and declared scope, the exact deterministic core characterizes the equivalence among fiber invariance, universal soundness of feature-difference inference, and descent through the target-induced quotient. The paper does not claim that this characterization discovers the target assignment, supplies scientific warrant, or establishes unrestricted numerical identity.

The methodological contribution is diagnostic: it separates the points at which an individuation inference must introduce target assignment, structural admissibility, observation-to-target bridging, independent scientific warrant, and permissible representational structure. The examples have different roles rather than forming independent case studies: bibliographic records motivate the problem, unique molecular identifier counting provides an experimental stress test, evidence synthesis shows methodological transfer, and the construct-level discussion is explicitly prospective. A small Lean 4 development is supplied as Online Resource 1 to verify dependency structure rather than to claim mathematical depth.
