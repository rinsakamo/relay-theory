# Author biography — Foundations of Science

Rintaro Sakamoto, MPH, is an independent scholar in Kitakyushu, Japan. He holds a Master of Public Health degree and works on methodological and formal questions concerning scientific representation, measurement, individuation, and cognition. His current research develops cross-disciplinary methods for distinguishing representational structure from target-level evidential claims and uses lightweight formal verification to make the dependencies of those arguments auditable.
