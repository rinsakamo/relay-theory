**Terminal classification:** `PAPER0_INTEGRATION_NOVELTY_ONLY`

## 1. Terminal assessment

The v0.2 manuscript survives as a **methodological integration / protocol paper**, not as a component-method novelty paper. The hostile audit has successfully removed the most vulnerable priority claims. The manuscript now explicitly treats conceptual explication, counterexample-guided abstraction and refinement, analyst-choice / robustness analysis, preregistration and Registered Reports, relational structure mapping, formal concept analysis, ontology matching, systematic-review provenance, measurement invariance, formal refinement and forgetting, severe testing, bibliometric science mapping, literature-based discovery, conceptual cartography, theory-label eliminativism, and formal theory comparison as prior art or direct near-neighbors.

No reviewed source establishes a complete collapse of the specific ordered contract defended in v0.2: adversarial construction of a bounded basis -> pre-outcome freeze of the representation/comparison contract -> source-grounded, construct-label/authority-blinded but source-semantics-grounded decomposition and mapping -> bounded equivalence/refinement/incomparability/residual semantics -> held-out checks for coverage, reproducibility, leakage, discrimination, and bespoke-encoding pressure. This is a **non-collapse result for the audited sources**, not evidence of historical firstness.

## 2. Strongest remaining collapse risks

### 2.1 Integrated-predecessor risk — still live

Because every major component has substantial precedent, discovery of a prior framework that already couples substantially the same stages would collapse the remaining novelty claim. The manuscript now states this explicitly and assigns the correct downgrade condition: `PAPER0_PRIOR_ART_COLLAPSE`.

**Assessment:** acceptable for a preprint, provided historical firstness and exhaustive-prior-art language remain absent.

### 2.2 Reproducibility of basis forging — unvalidated

The v0.2 contract is operationally sharper than v0.1: it defines a forging surface, candidate ledger, attack family, reconstruction and independence witnesses, a finite freeze point, and versioned amendments. But it has not yet shown that independent analysts converge sufficiently under those rules.

**Assessment:** unresolved empirical risk, not a drafting defect. It belongs to downstream qualification rather than to another round of terminology expansion.

### 2.3 Theory-ladenness of candidate generation — irreducible

The protocol exposes candidate-generation choices but cannot make them objective. v0.2 correctly says this. A reviewer can still ask whether the initial forging surface covertly determines the later atlas.

**Assessment:** adequately bounded in the manuscript. Future applications should report candidate provenance, alternative candidate sets where feasible, and analyst-disagreement surfaces.

### 2.4 Structural expressivity / vacuity — major scientific test remains

The manuscript now freezes schema and relation inventory, forbids claim-specific schema growth, tracks generic-rule reuse, distinguishes same-inventory/different-topology cases, and permits residuals instead of forced fits. These controls make the failure mode testable, but they do not prove that a concrete implementation will be non-vacuous.

**Assessment:** methodologically defensible; empirical success remains unearned.

### 2.5 Held-out validation — correctly narrowed

v0.1 risked sounding as though hold-out literature could validate the method in a broad sense. v0.2 instead limits held-out checks to one frozen system's mapping coverage, reproducibility, label/authority leakage resistance, discrimination, residual behavior, and bespoke-encoding pressure.

**Assessment:** corrected. The manuscript no longer treats held-out success as proof of truth, ontology completeness, or domain-generality.

### 2.6 Cross-domain generality — not established

The manuscript now treats portability as a future hypothesis requiring an independent-domain application. It does not infer domain-generality from the cognitive-science program.

**Assessment:** corrected.

## 3. Prior-art stress test after revision

The late hostile pass adds three particularly important philosophy-of-science neighbors:

- Charles H. Pence's **conceptual cartography** removes any novelty claim based on literature-facing cartography or digitally assisted mapping of concept use.
- Peter Vickers's **scientific theory eliminativism** removes any novelty claim based on escaping disputed named-theory categories as privileged analytical units.
- Toby Meadows's formal framework for **theory comparison** removes any novelty claim based on systematizing mathematical equivalence relations among theories.

These sources strengthen the negative novelty result but do not, on the inspected evidence, supply the complete Paper 0 ordered protocol. Their inclusion therefore supports `PAPER0_INTEGRATION_NOVELTY_ONLY`, not `PAPER0_METHOD_NOVELTY_SUPPORTED` and not yet `PAPER0_PRIOR_ART_COLLAPSE`.

## 4. Claim-discipline audit

The current manuscript passes the principal overclaim checks:

- no claim of a uniquely correct, complete, or metaphysically fundamental basis;
- no semantics-free interpretation claim;
- no claim that construct labels are literally unused in source comprehension;
- no historical-firstness claim;
- no comparative-superiority claim;
- no demonstrated cross-domain-validity claim;
- no claim that AI is scientific authority or methodological novelty;
- no inference from structural equivalence to semantic, causal, practical, or metaphysical identity;
- no use of an unpublished Paper 2 atlas outcome or Paper 3 validation outcome.

The manuscript's use of “integration novelty” remains vulnerable but is now stated conditionally and falsifiably.

## 5. Method-contract audit

The revision resolves the substantive underspecification identified in the hostile review to the level appropriate for a methodological preprint. In particular, it now provides:

- a declared forging surface and candidate-provenance ledger;
- an explicit attack family and witness discipline;
- a finite freeze point rather than an impossible exhaustive search requirement;
- a frozen versioned comparison contract;
- source anchors and abstention for unsupported decomposition;
- construct-label / authority masking without deleting source semantics;
- bounded representation and relation inventories;
- explicit equivalence, strict refinement with forgetting witness, incomparability, and typed residuals;
- decision ledgers for analyst degrees of freedom;
- amendment/version rules that prevent silent post-outcome retuning;
- generic-rule-reuse and bespoke-encoding diagnostics;
- held-out criteria tied to failure modes rather than to broad truth claims.

No additional machinery should be added merely to manufacture novelty.
